# Telegram Story Templates

## How to Add Templates

1. Create story designs in Canva (or any design tool)
2. Export as PNG or JPG
3. Recommended size: 1080x1920 (vertical) or 1080x1350 (square)
4. Save images here with names: `story-01.png`, `story-02.png`, `story-03.png`, etc.

## Design Tips

- Leave space for text overlay (central area)
- Use brand colors: Your English Corner theme
- Add subtle background patterns or gradients
- Include logo if desired (in corner)
- Keep designs clean and mobile-friendly

## What Happens

The bot will:
1. Randomly select one of your templates
2. Overlay the story text on the image
3. Post to Telegram channel
