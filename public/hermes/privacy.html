Privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-10 · Application: Hermes Personal Assistant

This policy applies to Kara Abdolmaleki’s private Hermes Personal Assistant application and its Google integrations. It is not the privacy policy for unrelated business websites or for every deployment of Hermes Agent.

1. Access and information

Only Google services and permissions expressly authorized by the account owner may be accessed. Depending on the granted scopes and a requested task, information may include account identity, Gmail messages and metadata (read-only), calendar events, contact details, and authorized Drive, Docs or Sheets content. OAuth credentials are stored to maintain that authorized connection. No Google connection is implied by this public website.

2. Permitted use

Google information is used solely to provide the owner’s requested user-facing assistant functions: email triage, scheduling, contact lookup, document assistance and task continuity. It is not sold, used for advertising, used for credit decisions, or used to develop or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Hermes Personal Assistant’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. A connection must not be enabled or used in a way that contradicts these restrictions.

3. Processing and disclosure

The application runs on Hostinger-hosted infrastructure. Authorized information may be processed by the owner’s configured AI model provider to fulfill a specific request. Such processing must be limited to the requested function, and Google data must not be sent to a provider unless its applicable terms and configuration prohibit using that data to train generalized models. No particular model provider is permanently fixed by this policy.

Information may be disclosed only as necessary to provide the authorized function, with the owner’s consent, for security purposes, or where legally required. It is not shared with unrelated businesses or made public. Human review is limited to the owner or authorized support/security review as permitted by Google’s policy; there is no advertising or data-broker access.

4. Storage and protection

OAuth credentials are stored in the application’s private, access-restricted server storage, not on these public pages. Google connections use HTTPS. Credentials must not be included in chat messages, public files or logs. There is no claim here of end-to-end encryption or a security certification.

Task records, requested outputs and conversation history may retain information needed for continuity. Security and operational logs may retain necessary metadata. Storage is limited to what the authorized function requires; retention does not authorize new use of the data.

5. Revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents continued authorized API access; it does not automatically erase prior conversation history, requested outputs or task records.

Contact the application owner to request removal of locally retained Google-derived information and saved credentials. Deletion requests are handled according to the data location and any applicable legal or security retention obligations. The application does not promise an unimplemented automatic retention period.

6. This public site

These informational pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, third-party fonts, embedded media or application-level tracking cookies. Hosting and network providers may process routine request logs under their own policies. The separate private dashboard has its own authentication/session mechanisms.

7. Changes and contact

This policy will be updated before material changes to Google data access or use. New Google permissions still require authorization. Questions and privacy requests: kara.abdolmaleki@gmail.com.
