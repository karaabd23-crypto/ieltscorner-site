# Session Note: 2026-10-05

## Purpose

Launch prep for the CELPIP Writing Guide, a companion ebook to the CELPIP Speaking Preparation Guide and the first title in a new product line.

## What Was Done

- Wrote the full manuscript (24 chapters, about 33,000 words, 27 sample answers at three levels, 30 sentence patterns, 12 common errors with drills, 60 practice prompts, 4-week plan). It covers the 2 real CELPIP Writing tasks: Task 1 email and Task 2 survey response.
- Built the sales page at `/writing-guide/`, modelled on `/ebook/` (same layout, classes and section order).

## Decisions

- **The manuscript is NOT in this repo.** The repo is public, so committing a paid product would publish it for free. It was delivered to Kay privately and saved to the claude.ai Project as `claude/CELPIP-Writing-Guide-manuscript-v1.md`.
- **The page is pre-launch.** `STRIPE_PAYMENT_LINK` and `PRICE_LABEL` at the top of `src/pages/writing-guide/index.astro` are empty placeholders. While either is empty the buy buttons read "Available soon", no price is shown, the page is `noindex`, and it is excluded from the sitemap.
- The floating CTA pill is hidden on `/writing-guide/`, matching `/ebook/`.
- Every number on the sales page was counted from the manuscript. Recount if the manuscript changes.
