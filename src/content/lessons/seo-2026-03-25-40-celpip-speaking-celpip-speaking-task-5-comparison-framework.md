<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead">CELPIP Speaking Task 5 asks you to compare two options and make a recommendation. The strongest responses give a direct comparison on two or three specific criteria, then commit to one option with a clear reason.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Weak</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>"Both options have advantages and disadvantages. It depends on the person."</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Stronger</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>"Option A is the better choice for a young professional because it is 40% cheaper and located 10 minutes from the downtown transit hub, whereas Option B costs more and requires a car. Unless you prioritise outdoor space, Option A is clearly the stronger value."</p>
  
</article>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Criterion-by-criterion</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Compare both options on the same feature before moving to the next</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"On cost, Option A is $800/month versus Option B at $1,100/month. On commute time, Option A is 15 minutes versus Option B at 45 minutes."</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Contrastive connectors</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Use "whereas", "while", "in contrast", "on the other hand"</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"Option A offers a shorter commute, whereas Option B provides more living space."</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Commit to one option</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">End with a clear recommendation, not "it depends"</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"For a commuter prioritising time, Option A is the stronger choice."</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Target audience qualifier</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Name who your recommendation best serves</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"For a family with children, Option B's larger garden outweighs the cost difference."</p>
</div>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">"It depends" non-answer</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>Both options are good, it really depends on what you want.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>For someone who commutes daily and wants to keep costs low, Option A is clearly preferable.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Name the target profile and commit. "It depends" is scored as a lack of Task Fulfilment.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Listing, not comparing</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>Option A has a pool. Option B has a balcony. Option A is near a park.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>Both options have outdoor amenities: Option A has a pool suited for summer use, while Option B's balcony offers year-round flexibility.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Put both options in the same sentence for each criterion to create a genuine comparison.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">No recommendation</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>I have described both options and they each have good features.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>Given the cost difference and commute advantage, Option A is the more practical choice for a first-time renter.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> The task requires a recommendation. Describe and then commit.</p>
</article>
</div>

## Why It Matters
<p class="lesson-importance">CELPIP Speaking Task 5 tests comparative reasoning and commitment. Candidates who say "it depends" without naming the dependency profile score CLB 7 on Task Fulfilment. The task explicitly asks for a recommendation, which requires choosing one option. A criterion-by-criterion comparison followed by a targeted recommendation ("for X, Option A is better because...") meets the CLB 9 standard for both Task Fulfilment and Coherence.</p>
