## What Can, Could, Be Able To Means
This lesson is specifically about **Can, Could, Be Able To**. By the end, you should understand when to use it, how to form it correctly, and how to avoid common errors.

## Topic Explanation and Use
Can, Could, Be Able To controls hypothetical vs real situations. The form shows whether a result is factual, probable, or imagined.

Use Can, Could, Be Able To by matching the if-clause form to the result clause so probability and timeline stay consistent.

Use conditions:
- Choose one conditional pattern and keep both clauses aligned.
- Use would/could/might in result clauses for unreal present/future meaning.
- Avoid mixing real and unreal patterns in one condition-result pair.
