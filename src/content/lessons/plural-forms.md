<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Plural Forms</strong> are essential for expressing quantity and number accurately in English. This lesson will help you understand how to form plurals correctly, ensuring your communication is clear and grammatically correct.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>She bought three book.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>She bought three <strong>books</strong>.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The noun 'book' needs to be plural to match the number 'three'.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>There are many cat in the yard.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>There are many <strong>cats</strong> in the yard.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">'Cat' should be plural to agree with 'many'.</p>
</article>
</div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>Plural forms in English are used to indicate that there is more than one of a noun. They are typically formed by adding <strong>-s</strong> or <strong>-es</strong> to the singular form, but there are many irregular plurals. Mastering plural forms is crucial at the B2 level to ensure clear and accurate communication.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Referring to more than one item, person, or concept.</li>
      <li>Discussing general categories or groups in writing and speech.</li>
      <li>Forming subject-verb agreement in sentences involving plural subjects.</li>
      <li>Writing academic or formal texts where precise language is required.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">The <strong>books</strong> are on the table.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">She has visited several <strong>countries</strong>.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">Many <strong>children</strong> enjoy playing outside.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Add <strong>-s</strong> to most nouns to make them plural.</li>
      <li>Use <strong>-es</strong> for nouns ending in <strong>-s, -sh, -ch, -x, or -z</strong>.</li>
      <li>Watch out for irregular plurals such as <strong>children</strong> and <strong>mice</strong>.</li>
      <li>Ensure subject-verb agreement with plural nouns.</li>
      <li>Remember that some nouns have the same form in both singular and plural, like <strong>sheep</strong>.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>Forgetting to add -s or -es for plurals</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>We need more chair for the event.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>We need more <strong>chairs</strong> for the event.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Add -s to 'chair' to make it plural.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>Using incorrect irregular plurals</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The childs are playing outside.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The <strong>children</strong> are playing outside.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use 'children' as the plural of 'child'.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>Subject-verb agreement with plural nouns</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The dogs runs fast.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The dogs <strong>run</strong> fast.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use 'run' to agree with the plural subject 'dogs'.</p>
</article>
</div>
