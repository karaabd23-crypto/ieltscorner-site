<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Collocations: Common Phrases</strong> focuses on mastering natural word combinations that enhance the fluency and precision of your English. This lesson introduces key collocations, explains their usage, and guides you in applying them effectively in various contexts.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Too weak</span>Students should do progress every week.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>Students should make progress every week.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The collocation 'make progress' is the correct and natural pairing in English.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Too weak</span>The team did a decision after the meeting.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The team made a decision after the meeting.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">'Make a decision' is the standard collocation, enhancing clarity and fluency.</p>
</article>
</div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>Collocations are specific combinations of words that frequently occur together, making your English sound more natural and fluent. They help convey precise meanings and are essential at the B2 level for effective communication in both spoken and written contexts.</p>
    <p>Examples include phrases like <strong>make progress</strong> and <strong>take responsibility</strong>. Understanding these combinations helps you choose the right words effortlessly.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Writing essays or reports where precise language is crucial.</li>
      <li>Engaging in professional or academic discussions.</li>
      <li>Preparing for exams like IELTS or CELPIP where lexical resource is assessed.</li>
      <li>Improving fluency in everyday conversations.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">Students <strong>make progress</strong> when they review consistently.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">Teams <strong>take responsibility</strong> for checking the final draft.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">She <strong>made a decision</strong> after considering all the facts.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Learn collocations as fixed expressions to improve fluency.</li>
      <li>Use collocations to convey precise meanings and avoid awkward phrasing.</li>
      <li>Practice using new collocations in sentences immediately to reinforce learning.</li>
      <li>Be aware of common collocation partners like 'make', 'do', 'take', etc.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>pairing a common noun with the wrong verb</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>We did research and then did a conclusion.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>We did research and then reached a conclusion.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Learn the full collocation instead of translating word by word.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>changing a familiar collocation because another verb feels similar</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>She took progress after practice.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>She made progress after practice.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Keep the partner verb English normally uses.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>memorizing the phrase but not using it in a sentence</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Take responsibility.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Students should take responsibility for checking their final answer.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Activate the collocation in a complete sentence.</p>
</article>
</div>
