## What Making Clear Arguments Means

## Topic Explanation and Use
Making Clear Arguments controls how a sentence carries meaning: reference, sequence, and emphasis must stay clear from start to finish.

Use Making Clear Arguments to make one precise meaning per sentence, then expand only when the added structure improves clarity for exam readers.

Use conditions:
- Choose the target form after deciding the exact meaning (fact, contrast, cause, or condition).
- Keep agreement and word order stable before adding extra clauses or modifiers.
- If a longer sentence becomes harder to scan, split it and keep the same logic.
