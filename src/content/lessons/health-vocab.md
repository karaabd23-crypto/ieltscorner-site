## How It Works

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>using general health language instead of the exact service term</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The clinic helped people before they got sick.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The clinic invested in <strong>preventive care</strong>.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> choose the exact health term for the service or action</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>using a technical term without showing what it does</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Screening is important.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Regular <strong>screening</strong> helps doctors identify illnesses earlier.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> add the real purpose or result after the term</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>using health words in a sentence with no clear result</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Treatment access and health outcome are needed.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Better <strong>treatment access</strong> can improve <strong>health outcomes</strong> in small towns.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> build the sentence around a clear action or effect</p>
</article>
</div>
