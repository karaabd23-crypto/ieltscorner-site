<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>For and Since in Time Expressions</strong> are essential for indicating the duration and starting points of actions or events. Understanding their correct usage allows for clear and precise communication, especially in academic and professional contexts. This lesson will guide you through their meanings, uses, and common errors.</p></div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>'For' and 'Since' are prepositions used in time expressions to indicate duration and starting points. 'For' specifies the length of time an action has been happening, while 'Since' indicates the starting point of an action that continues to the present.</p>
    <p>Formation: 'For' + duration (e.g., hours, days), 'Since' + specific starting point (e.g., a date, a time).</p>
    <p>Understanding these distinctions is crucial at the B2 level to ensure clarity in both spoken and written communication.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Describing how long an action has been occurring (e.g., 'for three years').</li>
      <li>Indicating when an action started and is still ongoing (e.g., 'since 2010').</li>
      <li>Writing formal reports or academic papers where precise time expressions are required.</li>
      <li>In spoken responses during exams to demonstrate time awareness.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">She has been working here <strong>for</strong> five years.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">They have lived in Canada <strong>since</strong> 2005.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Use 'for' with a duration (e.g., for two weeks).</li>
      <li>Use 'since' with a specific starting point (e.g., since last Monday).</li>
      <li>Do not mix 'for' with specific times or 'since' with durations.</li>
      <li>In perfect tenses, 'for' and 'since' are often used to show ongoing actions.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>Using 'for' with a specific time</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>He has been working here for 2015.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>He has been working here since 2015.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use 'since' with specific starting points.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>Using 'since' with a duration</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>They have lived here since ten years.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>They have lived here for ten years.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use 'for' with durations.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>Confusing 'for' and 'since' in perfect tenses</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>She has known him for 2009.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>She has known him since 2009.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use 'since' with specific starting points in perfect tenses.</p>
</article>
</div>
