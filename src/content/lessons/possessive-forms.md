<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Possessive Adjectives and Pronouns</strong> help you indicate ownership or relationships clearly without repeating the same noun. This lesson teaches you to use them effectively to enhance clarity and coherence in your communication.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>This is my book, and that is my book too.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>This is my book, and that is <strong>mine</strong> too.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">Replacing 'my book' with 'mine' avoids repetition and maintains clarity.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>Her coat is red, and her coat is blue.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span><strong>Her</strong> coat is red, and <strong>hers</strong> is blue.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">Using 'hers' instead of 'her coat' makes the sentence more concise.</p>
</article>
</div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>Possessive adjectives and pronouns indicate ownership or relationships. Possessive adjectives (my, your, his, her, its, our, their) modify nouns, while possessive pronouns (mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs) replace nouns to avoid repetition.</p>
    <p>These structures help in making sentences concise and clear by showing who owns what without repeating the noun.</p>
    <p>At the B2 level, mastering these helps in crafting precise and coherent sentences, especially in written communication.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Indicating ownership or relationships in a sentence.</li>
      <li>Replacing a noun to avoid repetition in a conversation or text.</li>
      <li>Writing formal letters or essays where clarity is crucial.</li>
      <li>Answering questions about personal belongings or relationships.</li>
      <li>Describing family, possessions, or attributes in spoken or written form.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence"><strong>Her</strong> car is faster than <strong>mine</strong>.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">This is <strong>our</strong> house, and that one is <strong>theirs</strong>.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Use possessive adjectives before a noun to show ownership.</li>
      <li>Use possessive pronouns to replace a noun and avoid repetition.</li>
      <li>Do not mix possessive adjectives with possessive pronouns.</li>
      <li>Ensure the pronoun or adjective matches the noun in number and gender.</li>
      <li>Possessive pronouns never require an apostrophe.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>confusing possessive adjectives with pronouns</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>This is her's book.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>This is <strong>her</strong> book.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use possessive adjectives without an apostrophe.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>using possessive pronouns incorrectly</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>That bag is her's.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>That bag is <strong>hers</strong>.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Possessive pronouns do not use apostrophes.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>mixing possessive adjectives and pronouns</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>That is my and his car.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>That is <strong>our</strong> car.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use 'our' to combine ownership.</p>
</article>
</div>
