<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Advanced Uses of 'Would'</strong> helps you express hypotheticals, past habits, polite requests, and reported future-in-the-past. Understanding the context is crucial as one form can carry different meanings.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>If she had more practice, she will perform better.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>If she had more practice, she would perform better.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">'Would' is used here for an unreal present conditional, indicating a hypothetical situation.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>When I was younger, I would played football every evening.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>When I was younger, I would play football every evening.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">'Would' is used for a past habitual action, requiring the base form of the verb.</p>
</article>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>The word <strong>would</strong> is a modal verb used to express various functions in English. It indicates hypothetical situations, describes past habits, makes polite requests, and reports future actions from a past perspective. Understanding these uses is crucial for B2 level learners to enhance clarity and express nuanced ideas.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Describing hypothetical results in conditional sentences (e.g., If I had more time, I <strong>would</strong> travel more).</li>
      <li>Talking about repeated actions or habits in the past (e.g., We <strong>would</strong> go hiking every summer).</li>
      <li>Making polite requests or offers in formal contexts (e.g., I <strong>would</strong> appreciate your feedback).</li>
      <li>Reporting a future intention from a past context (e.g., She said she <strong>would</strong> attend the meeting).</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">If the city invested more in transit, residents <strong>would save</strong> time every day.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">My teacher said the committee <strong>would review</strong> our proposal next week.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Always pair <strong>would</strong> with a base verb, not a past or infinitive form.</li>
      <li>In unreal conditionals, use <strong>would</strong> + base verb in the result clause.</li>
      <li>Use <strong>would</strong> for past habits only when the context is clear.</li>
      <li>In reported speech, use <strong>would</strong> to indicate future-in-the-past.</li>
      <li>Use <strong>would like</strong> for polite requests or offers.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>using will instead of would in unreal present conditionals</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>If I had more time, I will join the workshop.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>If I had more time, I would join the workshop.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> In unreal present conditionals, use 'would' + base verb in the result clause to indicate a hypothetical situation.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>using a past form after would in habitual past structures</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>When I was a child, I would played outside every evening.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>When I was a child, I would play outside every evening.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> After 'would', always use the base form of the verb for past habits.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>forgetting would in reported future-in-the-past</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>She said she will send the revised draft by Monday.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>She said she would send the revised draft by Monday.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Backshift 'will' to 'would' when reporting future actions from a past perspective.</p>
</article>
</div>
