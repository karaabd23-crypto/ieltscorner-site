<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Words Often Confused</strong> focuses on vocabulary pairs that are frequently mixed up due to similar appearance or pronunciation. This lesson helps you understand their distinct meanings and correct usage in context, enhancing both clarity and precision in communication.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Too weak</span>The new rule had a positive affect on attendance.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The new rule had a positive effect on attendance.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">'Effect' is the correct noun form needed here, while 'affect' is a verb.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Too weak</span>The policy will advice students about safety.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The policy will advise students about safety.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">'Advise' is the verb form, whereas 'advice' is a noun.</p>
</article>
</div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>Words Often Confused are pairs or groups of words that are frequently mistaken for one another due to similar spelling or pronunciation, but they have different meanings and uses.</p>
    <p>These words can change the meaning of a sentence if used incorrectly. Understanding their distinct meanings is crucial for clear communication.</p>
    <p>At the B2 level, mastering these distinctions helps in writing and speaking more precisely and professionally.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Writing essays or reports where precise vocabulary is required.</li>
      <li>Engaging in professional or academic communication.</li>
      <li>Preparing for language proficiency exams that test vocabulary usage.</li>
      <li>Editing written work to ensure clarity and correctness.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">The new rule had a positive <strong>effect</strong> on attendance.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">Rising fuel prices <strong>affect</strong> both workers and students.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">The teacher will <strong>advise</strong> you on your course selection.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Identify the part of speech required before selecting the word.</li>
      <li>Understand the specific meaning each word conveys in context.</li>
      <li>Practice using each word in a sentence to reinforce its correct use.</li>
      <li>Be aware of common collocations that can help clarify meaning.</li>
      <li>Review and contrast similar words regularly to avoid confusion.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>Choosing the wrong part of speech</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The teacher gave good advise before the exam.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The teacher gave good advice before the exam.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> 'Advice' is a noun, while 'advise' is a verb. Determine the needed part of speech first.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>Choosing the similar-looking word with the wrong meaning</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>This bus pass is economic for students.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>This bus pass is economical for students.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> 'Economical' means cost-effective, while 'economic' relates to the economy.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>Studying without contrasting sentences</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Affect/effect.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Fuel prices affect commuters, and one effect is lower car use.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Write sentences for each word to clarify their distinct meanings.</p>
</article>
</div>
