## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>IELTS Speaking Part 1 requires you to answer questions in a way that is clear, concise, and personal. Your responses should be structured to include a direct answer, a supporting detail, and possibly a brief example. This approach helps you sound natural and coherent, which is crucial for achieving a high score. At the B2 level, mastering this technique is important because it allows you to handle questions confidently and fluently under exam conditions.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Answering personal questions about your daily life, work, or hobbies.</li>
      <li>Responding to questions about preferences or opinions.</li>
      <li>Needing to provide a quick, coherent response under time pressure.</li>
      <li>Wanting to avoid sounding rehearsed or robotic.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence"><strong>Yes, I do.</strong> I usually take the bus because parking near my office is expensive.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence"><strong>Not really.</strong> I prefer reading at home because it helps me relax after work.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Start with a direct answer to the question.</li>
      <li>Follow up with one specific reason or example.</li>
      <li>Keep your language natural and personal.</li>
      <li>Avoid using memorized phrases or scripts.</li>
      <li>Conclude your response once it feels complete.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>giving an unclear personal answer</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Yes, maybe, depending on many things and different situations.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Yes, I do, because studying alone helps me focus without distraction.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Start with a direct personal answer, then add a specific detail.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>adding a generic reason</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>It is better because it is good for me.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>I usually study alone because I can control the pace and take short breaks when I need them.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Provide a personal reason that adds depth to your answer.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>speaking for too long without shape</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>There are many advantages and disadvantages, so it is difficult to explain everything now.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>That is why I usually choose to work on my own for short tasks.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Conclude your answer once it feels complete and coherent.</p>
</article>
</div>
