<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Prefixes</strong> are letters added to the beginning of a word to change its meaning. In this lesson, you will learn how common prefixes like <em>un-</em>, <em>mis-</em>, <em>dis-</em>, and <em>re-</em> help you express precise meanings in your communication.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>The instructions were clear, so everyone understood them.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The instructions were unclear, so not everyone understood them.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">'Unclear' uses the prefix 'un-' to indicate the opposite of 'clear.'</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>He wrote the report after receiving new data.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>He rewrote the report after receiving new data.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">'Rewrite' uses the prefix 're-' to indicate doing the action again.</p>
</article>
</div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>Prefixes are groups of letters added to the beginning of a word to alter its meaning. They can indicate negation, repetition, or an incorrect action, among other things. For example, adding 'un-' to 'happy' creates 'unhappy,' indicating the opposite meaning. At the B2 level, understanding prefixes helps you expand your vocabulary and express nuanced meanings more precisely.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>You need to express a negative or opposite meaning, such as 'unhappy' or 'disagree.'</li>
      <li>You want to indicate repetition of an action, like 'rewrite' or 'redo.'</li>
      <li>You need to convey an incorrect action, such as 'misunderstand.'</li>
      <li>In formal writing or speaking, where precise word choice is crucial.</li>
      <li>When preparing for exams like IELTS or CELPIP that test vocabulary range.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">The project was <strong>unfinished</strong> due to unforeseen circumstances.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">He decided to <strong>revisit</strong> the proposal after receiving feedback.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Use 'un-', 'in-', 'dis-' for negation or opposite meanings.</li>
      <li>Use 're-' to indicate repetition of an action.</li>
      <li>Ensure the prefixed word is commonly used in English.</li>
      <li>Check that the new word fits the context of your sentence.</li>
      <li>Avoid inventing new words; rely on established vocabulary.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>using the base word where the sentence needs a prefix meaning</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The manager asked us to check the data again, so we had to view the file before submission.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The manager asked us to check the data again, so we had to review the file before submission.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use 're-' to indicate repetition of an action.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>using a positive base form when the context needs a negative prefix</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The online form is possible to complete without an ID.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The online form is impossible to complete without an ID.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use 'im-' to negate the base meaning.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>forgetting re- when the action is repeated</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Please write your answer after checking the teacher comments.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Please rewrite your answer after checking the teacher comments.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use 're-' when the action clearly means to do again.</p>
</article>
</div>
