## What this lesson covers

IELTS Listening Distractor Detection System gives you a practical system for IELTS listening. You will see how to prepare before the audio plays, what to focus on while listening, and how to verify your answers.

<Callout type="tip" title="How to use this lesson">
Study this lesson before your next practice test. During the test, apply one strategy from the How It Works section. After the test, check which questions you got wrong and identify the pattern.
</Callout>

## Examples

<div class="lesson-grid">
  <div class="panel">
    <h3>Weak approach</h3>
    <p>Wait for the audio to start, then write down everything you hear. When the speaker changes topic, lose track of the question. Choose the first answer that mentions the keyword.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel">
    <h3>Stronger approach</h3>
    <p>Before the audio starts, read the questions and predict the answer type (a name, a number, a reason). While listening, track the question number. When the speaker corrects themselves, write the corrected version, not the first one.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
  <h3>Rules for every listening section</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Read all questions during the preparation time -- do not wait for audio.</li>
    <li>Keep your pen on the question paper to track where you are.</li>
    <li>When a speaker self-corrects, always write the corrected version.</li>
    <li>If you miss an answer, move to the next question immediately.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Errors that cost points">
1. Writing the first number or name you hear -- speakers often correct themselves.
2. Falling behind by focusing on one missed answer -- move on.
3. Spelling errors on names and addresses -- listen for spelling out loud (B for Baker...).
</Callout>

## Why It Matters

Every listening question you answer correctly adds directly to your band score. The two most preventable causes of listening score loss are distractor errors (choosing the wrong version of an answer) and falling behind after a missed question. The system in this lesson addresses both directly.
