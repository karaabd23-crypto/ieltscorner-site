## Two meanings you must separate

### 1) Topic meaning (about = concerning)
- This essay is **about** climate policy.

### 2) Approximation meaning (about / around = approximately)
- **About/Around** 60% of residents commute by car.

<Callout type="warning" title="IELTS writing issue">
"Around" can sound slightly informal in Task 2.  
Band 7+ often uses: <strong>approximately</strong>, <strong>roughly</strong>, <strong>in the region of</strong>.
</Callout>

<PatternTable
  caption="Common IELTS-friendly approximators"
  rows={[
    { pattern: "approximately + number/%", meaning: "formal, precise approximation", example: "Approximately 35% of households rent." },
    { pattern: "roughly + number/%", meaning: "semi-formal, common in Task 1/2", example: "Roughly half of respondents disagreed." },
    { pattern: "in the region of + number", meaning: "formal estimate", example: "The cost is in the region of $2,000 per month." }
  ]}

## Practice: choose the best option

Rewrite with a more academic approximator:

1) Around 70% of people use smartphones.  
2) The policy costs about $5 billion.  
3) The lecture was about AI. (keep "topic" meaning)
