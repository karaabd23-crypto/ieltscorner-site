## What is an abstract noun?

An abstract noun is a word for an idea, not a physical thing. Words like **growth**, **risk**, **pressure**, and **freedom** are abstract nouns.

In IELTS writing, abstract nouns can make your style more academic. But keep sentences short and clear, so the meaning is easy to follow.

<div class="lesson-grid">
  <div class="panel">
    <h3>Concrete style</h3>
    <p>Many people are poor in cities.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel">
    <h3>Abstract noun style</h3>
    <p>Urban poverty remains a serious problem.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<Callout type="tip" title="Easy upgrade">
Use one abstract noun per sentence, not many. This keeps your writing formal but readable.
</Callout>

## Useful patterns

<PatternTable
  caption="Patterns for Task 2"
  rows={[
    { pattern: "noun + is + adjective", meaning: "describe an idea", example: "Social pressure is intense in large cities." },
    { pattern: "noun + has increased/decreased", meaning: "show change", example: "Public awareness has increased in recent years." },
    { pattern: "noun + leads to + noun", meaning: "show cause and result", example: "Income inequality leads to social tension." }
  ]}
/>

<details class="panel">
  <summary><strong>When NOT to use abstract nouns</strong></summary>
  <p>If your sentence already sounds heavy, use a simple verb and a concrete example. Balance is better than difficult style.</p>
</details>

## Common mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Common learner problems">
- Using too many abstract nouns in one sentence.  
- Choosing the wrong noun form (for example, *develop* vs *development*).  
- Forgetting to explain the idea with a real example.
</Callout>

## Practice

1) Rewrite: *People are aware more now.*  
2) Rewrite: *When people are poor, there are many social problems.*  
3) Add one example to this sentence: *Economic growth is uneven.*
