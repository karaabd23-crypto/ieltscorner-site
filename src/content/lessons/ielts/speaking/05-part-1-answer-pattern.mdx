## What this lesson covers

IELTS Speaking Part 1 lasts 4-5 minutes and covers familiar topics: your hometown, work or study, hobbies, daily routines. Answers should be 2-4 sentences — long enough to show vocabulary and fluency, short enough to leave time for the examiner's next question. The most common error is either a one-word answer ("Yes") or an answer so long it resembles a Part 2 monologue.

## Examples

<div class="lesson-grid">
  <div class="panel">
    <h3>Weak</h3>
    <p>Do you enjoy cooking? "Yes. I like cooking."</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel">
    <h3>Stronger</h3>
    <p>"I do, actually. I find it a good way to decompress after a long day — I tend to cook fairly simple things like stir-fries or pasta, but I enjoy the process of putting a meal together from scratch."</p>
  </div>
</div>

The strong answer uses a natural opener ("I do, actually"), gives a reason (decompress after work), adds a concrete example (stir-fries, pasta), and ends on the process itself rather than just the outcome. It sounds like natural conversation, not a rehearsed script.

## How It Works

<PatternTable
  caption="Core patterns"
  rows={[
    { pattern: "Direct answer + \"actually/to be honest\"", meaning: "Add a natural hedge that sounds conversational", example: "I do, actually. / Not particularly, to be honest. / It depends, really." },
    { pattern: "Reason + specific example", meaning: "Give one reason and one concrete detail — no more", example: "\"I enjoy it because it helps me switch off. I usually try a new recipe on weekends.\"" },
    { pattern: "Personal feeling + brief extension", meaning: "Share how something makes you feel, then extend with a comparison or contrast", example: "\"I find it quite therapeutic, actually — much more so than I expected when I first started.\"" }
  ]}
/>

<section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
  <h3>Use this when</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>When the examiner asks about hobbies, routines, preferences, or experiences.</li>
    <li>When you want to extend naturally without sounding scripted.</li>
    <li>When you have given a short answer and the examiner is waiting — add the "because/actually/I find that" extension.</li>
  </ul>

## Common Mistakes

| Avoid | Prefer | Why |
|-------|--------|-----|
| "Yes, I like my hometown because it is a very nice place with many things." | "I have a lot of affection for it, actually. It is a fairly quiet city, but there is a strong community feel that you do not always find in larger places." | "Nice" and "many things" are filler. Describe what specifically makes it nice. |
| Answering every question with a memorised script. | Answering directly, then extending naturally. | Memorised answers often sound unnatural and disconnect from the specific question asked. React to each question genuinely. |
| "I am not sure. Maybe yes. I think so. It depends." | "It depends on the season, really. I tend to prefer it in summer when there is more going on." | "It depends" is a valid start — but always follow it with what it depends on and which side you favour. |

## Why It Matters

Part 1 answers contribute to your Fluency and Coherence score. Examiners assess whether you can produce connected speech without long pauses, repetition, or self-correction. A two-sentence answer that flows naturally scores higher than a long answer filled with "um," "uh," and false starts. Aim for 2-4 sentences per question, no more.
