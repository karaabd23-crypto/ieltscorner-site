<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Mixed Conditionals</strong> involve combining two different conditional forms to express complex relationships between time and events. This lesson will help you understand how to use mixed conditionals to describe scenarios where past conditions affect present results or vice versa, enhancing your ability to communicate nuanced ideas effectively.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>If I had known about the meeting, I would attend it now.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>If I had known about the meeting, I would be attending it now.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The correction aligns the past condition with the present result using mixed conditionals.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>If she were more careful, she would have avoided the mistake yesterday.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>If she had been more careful, she would not have made the mistake yesterday.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">This version correctly uses mixed conditionals to show past conditions affecting past results.</p>
</article>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p><strong>Mixed Conditionals</strong> combine different types of conditional sentences to express complex time relationships between conditions and results. They often link past conditions with present results or vice versa.</p>
    <p>In English, mixed conditionals allow speakers to describe hypothetical scenarios where the time frame of the condition does not match the time frame of the result.</p>
    <p>At the B2 level, mastering mixed conditionals helps in expressing nuanced ideas and understanding complex texts.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Describing a present result of a past condition.</li>
      <li>Explaining a past hypothetical scenario affecting the present.</li>
      <li>Discussing regrets or missed opportunities with current implications.</li>
      <li>Writing essays or reports that require complex conditional reasoning.</li>
      <li>Engaging in discussions that involve hypothetical thinking.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">If I <strong>had studied</strong> harder, I <strong>would have a better job</strong> now.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">If she <strong>were more organized</strong>, she <strong>wouldn't have missed</strong> the deadline last week.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Use past perfect in the if-clause for past conditions affecting present results.</li>
      <li>Use conditional perfect in the result clause for present conditions affecting past results.</li>
      <li>Ensure the time frames of condition and result logically differ.</li>
      <li>Mixed conditionals often express hypothetical or unreal situations.</li>
      <li>Check the logic and time consistency between condition and result.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>Confusing past and present timelines</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>If I had more time, I would have finished the project last week.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>If I had had more time, I would have finished the project last week.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use past perfect in the if-clause for past conditions affecting past results.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>Mixing present and past without intention</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>If he knew the rules, he would have won the game yesterday.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>If he had known the rules, he would have won the game yesterday.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Ensure the if-clause reflects a past condition affecting a past result.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>Using future forms incorrectly</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>If I knew about the opportunity, I would take it tomorrow.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>If I had known about the opportunity, I would take it tomorrow.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use past perfect in the if-clause for mixed conditional scenarios.</p>
</article>
</div>
