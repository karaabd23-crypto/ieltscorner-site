<div class="lesson-context">
  <p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Past Perfect Continuous</strong> helps you express actions that were ongoing in the past and emphasize their duration or continuity before another past event. This tense is essential for creating detailed narratives and showing cause and effect in past timelines.</p>
</div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>She was tired because she worked all night.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>She was tired because she <strong>had been working</strong> all night.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">Use the Past Perfect Continuous to emphasize the duration of the action that caused her tiredness.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>They argued for hours before they reached an agreement.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>They <strong>had been arguing</strong> for hours before they reached an agreement.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The Past Perfect Continuous highlights the ongoing nature of the argument.</p>
</article>
</div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>The <strong>Past Perfect Continuous</strong> tense describes actions that were ongoing in the past and continued up to a specific past moment. It emphasizes the duration or continuity of an action before another past event. The structure is: <strong>subject + had been + verb-ing</strong>.</p>
    <p>This tense is important at the B2 level for crafting detailed narratives and explaining causes and effects in past timelines.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Describing an action that was ongoing before another past action or time.</li>
      <li>Emphasizing the duration of a past activity that was interrupted.</li>
      <li>Setting the scene in a narrative to show what was happening over time.</li>
      <li>Explaining the cause of a past situation or event.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">She <strong>had been working</strong> at the company for five years before she got promoted.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">They <strong>had been discussing</strong> the project for hours when the meeting ended.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Use 'had been' + verb-ing to form the Past Perfect Continuous.</li>
      <li>Always pair with another past action or time reference.</li>
      <li>Emphasize the duration or continuity of the action.</li>
      <li>Use it to explain the background or cause of a past event.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>Using simple past instead of past perfect continuous</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>He was exhausted because he ran all morning.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>He was exhausted because he <strong>had been running</strong> all morning.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use Past Perfect Continuous to emphasize the ongoing nature of the action.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>Ignoring the duration aspect of the past action</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>She was upset because she waited for a long time.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>She was upset because she <strong>had been waiting</strong> for a long time.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use Past Perfect Continuous to show the duration of the waiting.</p>
</article>
</div>
