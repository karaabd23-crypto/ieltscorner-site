## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Too weak</span>The city made dirty air lower with new rules.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The city cut <strong>emissions</strong> and improved air quality with new rules.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The stronger version uses precise vocabulary to clearly convey the environmental action taken.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">

  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Too weak</span>The country used cleaner power and that was better for nature.</p>

  <p class="lesson-card-note">The improved sentence uses specific terms to describe the action and its impact on sustainability.</p>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>using broad environmental words instead of the exact issue</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The city solved the pollution problem.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The city reduced <strong>vehicle emissions</strong> in the centre.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> name the exact environmental issue directly</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>using a topic word without cause, effect, or solution</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Air quality is important.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Air quality improved after diesel buses were replaced.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> attach the term to a real cause or result</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>mixing environmental terms loosely</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Renewable energy made conservation in the traffic system.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Renewable energy reduced the city's reliance on fossil fuels.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> use the topic word in a sentence that matches its real meaning</p>
</article>
</div>
