<div class="lesson-context">
  <p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Used to for past habits and states</strong> is a crucial structure for expressing actions or conditions that were true in the past but are no longer applicable. This lesson will guide you in using 'used to' effectively to describe past routines and states.</p>
</div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>He use to go to the gym every day.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>He <strong>used to</strong> go to the gym every day.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The correction uses 'used to' to correctly indicate a past habit.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>They used to going to the beach every weekend.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>They <strong>used to</strong> go to the beach every weekend.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">'Used to' should be followed by the base form of the verb.</p>
</article>
</div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>The phrase <strong>'used to'</strong> is used to describe actions or states that were true in the past but are no longer the case. It helps convey past habits or conditions in a clear manner.</p>
    <p>Formation: subject + <strong>'used to'</strong> + base verb.</p>
    <p>This structure is important at the B2 level as it aids in expressing past experiences accurately.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Describing past habits that no longer occur, e.g., "I <strong>used to</strong> jog every morning."</li>
      <li>Talking about past states that have changed, e.g., "She <strong>used to</strong> live in New York."</li>
      <li>Recounting past routines in storytelling or narratives.</li>
      <li>In formal writing to describe historical or personal changes over time.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">I <strong>used to</strong> play the piano when I was a child.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">They <strong>used to</strong> travel to Europe every summer.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Always follow <strong>'used to'</strong> with a base verb.</li>
      <li>Use <strong>'used to'</strong> for actions or states that are no longer true.</li>
      <li>Do not confuse with 'be used to', which means being accustomed to something.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>Using 'use to' instead of 'used to'</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>She use to work here.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>She <strong>used to</strong> work here.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Always use 'used to' for past habits or states.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>Incorrect verb form after 'used to'</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>We used to playing soccer in the park.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>We <strong>used to</strong> play soccer in the park.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Follow 'used to' with the base form of the verb.</p>
</article>
</div>
