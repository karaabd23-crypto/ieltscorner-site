<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Passives</strong> change the focus of the sentence from the doer to the action or result. This lesson will help you understand when using the passive voice is advantageous and when the active voice might be more effective.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>People recycle more plastic in this city every year.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>More plastic <strong>is recycled</strong> in this city every year.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The passive voice is used here to emphasize the action rather than the people doing it.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>The committee will announce the results tomorrow.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The results <strong>will be announced</strong> tomorrow.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">This passive construction highlights the results rather than the committee.</p>
</article>
</div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>The passive voice shifts the focus from the subject performing the action to the action itself or its recipient. It is formed using the verb 'be' followed by a past participle. This structure is crucial at the B2 level to enhance clarity and formality in writing.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>The action is more important than the doer.</li>
      <li>The doer is unknown or irrelevant.</li>
      <li>In formal or academic writing where objectivity is required.</li>
      <li>To emphasize the result of an action.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence"><strong>More buses were added</strong> to the route last winter.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence"><strong>The final timetable will be announced</strong> on Friday.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Use 'be' + past participle to form the passive.</li>
      <li>Include the agent with 'by' only if necessary for clarity.</li>
      <li>Switch to active voice if it clarifies responsibility.</li>
      <li>Ensure the tense of 'be' matches the intended time frame.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>Using an active verb form where passive focus is required</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The final report publish next Monday.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The final report <strong>will be published</strong> next Monday.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use 'be' + past participle to correctly form the passive.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>Forgetting the auxiliary verb in a passive sentence</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The final decision announced yesterday.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The final decision <strong>was announced</strong> yesterday.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Passive forms need a form of 'be' plus the past participle.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>Using passive voice when the active sentence is clearer</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Mistakes were made by the planning team during the launch.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The planning team <strong>made mistakes</strong> during the launch.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Switch back to active voice when responsibility matters.</p>
</article>
</div>
