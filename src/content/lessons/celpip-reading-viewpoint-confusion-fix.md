## Intro
Viewpoint confusion is common in CELPIP Part 4. Students read a quoted opinion and treat it as the author's final position.

## Examiner Criteria
- Distinguish who said what.
- Identify the author's final stance accurately.
- Use discourse markers to track contrast and evaluation.

## Mistakes
- Ignoring attribution words like "according to", "researchers claim", or "the author argues".
- Treating every sentence as equal weight.
- Missing contrast markers that signal the writer's real position.

## Strategy
1. Label each claim as Author, Source, or General Fact.
2. Highlight contrast words: but, however, although, despite.
3. Base final answers on the author's evaluative lines, not quoted examples.

## Example
Source claim: "Some analysts believe remote work lowers productivity."
Author stance: "However, current data suggests hybrid teams maintain output."

Correct viewpoint: The author supports hybrid productivity, not the analysts' concern.

## Exercise
1. Read one opinion passage and label each paragraph as Author, Source, or Fact.
2. Find two contrast markers.
3. Write one sentence describing the final author viewpoint.
