<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead">CELPIP Speaking Task 1 asks you to give advice to a friend. High-scoring responses give one clear recommendation, one concrete reason, and one practical next step. Vague advice and over-qualification drop the Coherence score.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Weak</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>"Well, I think maybe you could try to look for a job or maybe talk to someone about your situation and see what happens."</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Stronger</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>"My advice is to speak with your manager directly this week. In my experience, most scheduling conflicts are resolved in a single five-minute conversation, and waiting only makes the situation worse."</p>
  
</article>
</div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Clear recommendation</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">State the advice in the first sentence, not the third</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"I would recommend applying for the position before the Friday deadline."</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">One strong reason</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Give one specific reason rather than a list of vague ones</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"The main reason is that early applicants receive priority screening in most companies."</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Practical next step</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Tell them exactly what to do and when</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"Tonight, update your CV and send it to the HR email listed on the posting."</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Confident tone</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Avoid "maybe", "possibly", "I am not sure but"</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"I would definitely speak to your landlord before involving the rental authority."</p>
</div>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Delayed recommendation</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>There are many things to consider in this situation. First you should think about your options...</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>I would recommend speaking with your supervisor directly.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> State the recommendation in the first sentence. Task 1 is 30-40 seconds. Delay wastes your response time.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Multiple vague options</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>You could try option A or maybe option B or possibly option C.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>I suggest option A because it directly addresses the core issue.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Pick one option and defend it. Listing options without commitment sounds uncertain.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Over-hedging</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>I am not completely sure, but maybe you could possibly consider...</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>I would suggest applying early.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Remove "maybe", "possibly", and "I am not sure". Confident phrasing scores higher on Task Fulfilment.</p>
</article>
</div>

## Why It Matters
<p class="lesson-importance">CELPIP Speaking Task 1 is scored on Task Fulfilment, Coherence and Cohesion, and Vocabulary and Grammar. A response that gives a clear recommendation in the first sentence, supports it with one specific reason, and closes with a practical next step achieves the Task Fulfilment requirement efficiently and leaves speaking time for elaboration rather than preamble.</p>
