<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Prepositions of Time</strong> are essential in English to specify when events occur. They provide clarity and precision in communication, which is crucial for effective interaction in both professional and academic settings. This lesson will focus on their correct usage, offer practical examples, and identify common mistakes to avoid.</p></div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>Prepositions of time are used to indicate when an event occurs. They help specify time frames, dates, and durations. Common prepositions of time include <strong>at</strong>, <strong>on</strong>, and <strong>in</strong>. Mastery of these prepositions is important at the B2 level to enhance clarity in both written and spoken communication.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Describing specific times or moments (e.g., <strong>at</strong> 5 p.m.)</li>
      <li>Referring to days and dates (e.g., <strong>on</strong> Monday)</li>
      <li>Indicating months, years, or longer periods (e.g., <strong>in</strong> 2020)</li>
      <li>Talking about the duration of an event (e.g., <strong>for</strong> two hours)</li>
      <li>Specifying time limits or deadlines (e.g., <strong>by</strong> next week)</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">The meeting is scheduled <strong>at</strong> 3 p.m.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">We will meet <strong>on</strong> Friday.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">She was born <strong>in</strong> 1995.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Use <strong>at</strong> for specific times.</li>
      <li>Use <strong>on</strong> for days and dates.</li>
      <li>Use <strong>in</strong> for months, years, and longer periods.</li>
      <li>Remember that <strong>by</strong> indicates a deadline.</li>
      <li>Avoid using <strong>at</strong>, <strong>on</strong>, <strong>in</strong> interchangeably; each has a specific context.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>Using "in" instead of "at" for specific times</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The train leaves in 7 p.m.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The train leaves at 7 p.m.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use <strong>at</strong> for specific times.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>Using "in" instead of "on" for days</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>We have a meeting in Monday.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>We have a meeting on Monday.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use <strong>on</strong> for days and dates.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>Using "on" instead of "in" for years</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>He graduated on 2010.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>He graduated in 2010.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use <strong>in</strong> for years.</p>
</article>
</div>
