<div class="lesson-context">
  <p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>IELTS Writing Task 2: Advantage/Disadvantage</strong> involves comparing the positive and negative aspects of a topic. A strong response organizes ideas, explains their impact, and concludes with an evaluation. In this lesson, you will learn how to effectively balance both sides without being repetitive.</p>
</div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Too weak</span>This approach seems useful because it helps people in several ways.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>A major advantage of online learning is schedule flexibility for working students.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The stronger sentence provides a specific advantage and sets a clear direction for the paragraph.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Too weak</span>For example, one local program made services better for residents.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>However, one serious disadvantage is the weaker sense of accountability when learners study without regular supervision.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The stronger version develops the idea by explaining the disadvantage in detail.</p>
</article>
</div>


## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>In IELTS Writing Task 2: Advantage/Disadvantage, you are required to discuss both the positive and negative aspects of a given topic. This task assesses your ability to present balanced arguments and evaluate them effectively.</p>
    <p>To construct a strong response, identify the main advantages and disadvantages, explain their impacts, and conclude with an overall evaluation. This approach enhances clarity and coherence, which are crucial at the B2 proficiency level.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Responding to IELTS Task 2 prompts that ask about benefits and drawbacks.</li>
      <li>Writing essays that require balanced viewpoints and evaluations.</li>
      <li>Organizing ideas logically to enhance clarity and coherence.</li>
      <li>Concluding essays with a decisive evaluation of the discussed points.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence"><strong>A major advantage</strong> of online learning is schedule flexibility for working students.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence"><strong>However, one serious disadvantage</strong> is the weaker sense of accountability when learners study without regular supervision.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Identify and list the advantages and disadvantages before writing.</li>
      <li>Select one or two major points for each side to focus on.</li>
      <li>Explain the impact of each point on specific groups or systems.</li>
      <li>Use comparative language to weigh the sides in your conclusion.</li>
      <li>Ensure your overall judgment is clear and supported by your analysis.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>Listing advantages and disadvantages without developing either</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>There are many advantages. Also, there are disadvantages. It depends.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The main advantage is increased efficiency: companies can automate routine tasks, freeing staff for creative work.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> For each advantage or disadvantage, add a "why" or "how" explanation before moving to the next.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>Writing an unbalanced essay that only addresses one side</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>There are so many advantages that it is hard to see any disadvantages.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Although automation reduces some jobs, it creates new roles in maintenance and software development.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Dedicate at least one paragraph to each side — use "although" or "however" to signal the contrast.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>Using general nouns ('people', 'society') instead of specific groups</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>This helps people and society in many ways.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Remote workers, in particular, benefit from reduced daily commute time.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Replace "people" with a specific group: workers, students, elderly residents, local businesses.</p>
</article>
</div>
