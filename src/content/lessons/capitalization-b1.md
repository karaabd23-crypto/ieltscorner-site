## Goal
Learn how to use **Capitalization Rules** accurately in IELTS and CELPIP responses.

## What Capitalization Rules Means
This lesson is specifically about **Capitalization Rules**. By the end, you should understand when to use it, how to form it correctly, and how to avoid common errors.

## Topic Explanation and Use
Capitalization Rules controls how a sentence carries meaning: reference, sequence, and emphasis must stay clear from start to finish.

Use Capitalization Rules to make one precise meaning per sentence, then expand only when the added structure improves clarity for exam readers.

Use conditions:
- Choose the target form after deciding the exact meaning (fact, contrast, cause, or condition).
- Keep agreement and word order stable before adding extra clauses or modifiers.
- If a longer sentence becomes harder to scan, split it and keep the same logic.

Reference examples:
- *In exam writing, accurate Capitalization Rules helps each sentence carry one clear grammatical purpose.*
- *When Capitalization Rules is chosen deliberately, arguments stay precise and easier to evaluate under time pressure.*

3. Check agreement and tense around **Capitalization Rules** before adding extra words.
