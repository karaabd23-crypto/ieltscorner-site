## What this lesson covers

CELPIP Reading Time Control by Question Type gives you a repeatable system for answering CELPIP reading questions accurately under time pressure. You will see which steps to follow, which errors to avoid, and how to check your answers quickly.

<Callout type="tip" title="How to study this">
Try one practice reading question before reading this lesson. Note where you made errors. Then study the How It Works section and apply the system to the same question type again.
</Callout>

## Examples

<div class="lesson-grid">
  <div class="panel">
    <h3>Weak approach</h3>
    <p>Read the whole passage first, then go back to find answers. Spend equal time on every question. When uncertain, guess from memory.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel">
    <h3>Stronger approach</h3>
    <p>Read the question first. Identify the keyword. Scan the passage for that keyword or its paraphrase. Verify the surrounding sentence matches the question meaning before choosing.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## How It Works

<section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
  <h3>Steps to follow for every question</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Step 1: Read the question and underline the most specific keyword.</li>
    <li>Step 2: Scan the passage for that keyword or its synonym.</li>
    <li>Step 3: Read the two sentences around the keyword carefully.</li>
    <li>Step 4: Verify the answer matches the question meaning, not just the keyword.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Errors that cost points">
1. Choosing an answer because it repeats a word from the question -- the passage often paraphrases, not repeats.
2. Spending more than 90 seconds on one question -- move on and return if time allows.
3. Answering from general knowledge instead of the passage text.
</Callout>

## Why It Matters

CELPIP reading tests your ability to locate specific information and understand implied meaning quickly. Every question you answer correctly adds directly to your band score. The question-first system in this lesson prevents the two most common causes of score loss: spending too long on one question and choosing answers based on surface keyword matching rather than meaning.
