<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Past Perfect Simple</strong> is essential for clearly communicating the sequence of past events. It allows you to indicate which action happened first, providing clarity and precision in both writing and speaking. This lesson will help you use this tense accurately to enhance your communication skills.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>By the time we arrived, the movie starts.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>By the time we arrived, the movie <strong>had started</strong>.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The Past Perfect Simple tense indicates the movie started before we arrived.</p>
</article>

  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>She left after he had arrived.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>She <strong>had left</strong> before he arrived.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The Past Perfect Simple tense shows she left before his arrival.</p>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>The <strong>Past Perfect Simple</strong> tense is used to describe an action that was completed before another action in the past. It emphasizes the completion of the action before a specific time or event. The structure is: <strong>subject + had + past participle</strong>. Understanding this tense is crucial at the B2 level to clarify sequences of events in past narratives.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Describing an action completed before another past action, e.g., "She <strong>had left</strong> before he arrived."</li>
      <li>Providing background information in narratives, e.g., "By the time the meeting started, everyone <strong>had read</strong> the report."</li>
      <li>Reporting past experiences that influenced later events, e.g., "He felt confident because he <strong>had prepared</strong> thoroughly."</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">She <strong>had finished</strong> her homework before dinner.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">By the time we arrived, the movie <strong>had started</strong>.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Use <strong>had + past participle</strong> to form the Past Perfect Simple.</li>
      <li>Always pair it with a past reference point to show the sequence.</li>
      <li>Do not use it for actions that are not completed before another past action.</li>
      <li>Check that the past participle is correctly formed for irregular verbs.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>Using simple past instead of past perfect</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>After the guests arrived, she served dinner.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>After the guests <strong>had arrived</strong>, she served dinner.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use Past Perfect Simple to show the guests arrived before she served dinner.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>Confusing past perfect with present perfect</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>She has finished her work before the deadline.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>She <strong>had finished</strong> her work before the deadline.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use Past Perfect Simple for actions completed before a past point.</p>
</article>
</div>
