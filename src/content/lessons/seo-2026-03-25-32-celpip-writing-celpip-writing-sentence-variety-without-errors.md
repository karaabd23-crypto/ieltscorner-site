<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead">Sentence variety lifts your CELPIP Writing score from CLB 8 to CLB 9 on Linguistic Range. But adding complex structures only helps if they are accurate. This lesson shows you how to vary sentences safely.</p></div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Subordinate clause opener</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Move a "because/although/since" clause to the front</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"Because traffic is heavy, I prefer to telecommute."</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Relative clause</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Add detail with "which/who/that"</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"The policy, which was introduced last year, reduced absenteeism by 15%."</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Paired connector</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Use "not only ... but also" or "both ... and"</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"Remote work saves both time and money."</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Short sentence after long</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Follow a complex sentence with a short one for emphasis</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"After months of planning and three rounds of revision, the report was finally complete. It changed everything."</p>
</div>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Comma splice</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>I enjoy working remotely, it helps me focus.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>I enjoy working remotely because it helps me focus.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Two independent clauses cannot be joined with only a comma. Use a conjunction or a semicolon.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Overlong sentence</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>Working from home is good because you save time and money and you can also focus better and you do not need to wear formal clothes.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>Working from home saves time and money. It also allows you to focus in a quieter space without the pressure of office dress codes.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Break a chain of "and" clauses into two or three targeted sentences.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Fragment</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>Although remote work has many benefits.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>Although remote work has many benefits, it requires strong self-discipline.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> A clause beginning with "although/because/since" cannot stand alone. Add a main clause.</p>
</article>
</div>

## Why It Matters
<p class="lesson-importance">CELPIP Linguistic Range at CLB 9 rewards candidates who demonstrate control of complex sentence structures. The risk is that adding complexity also introduces errors. The safest path is to master one new structure at a time, verify it is accurate, and build it into your writing routine before attempting the next.</p>
