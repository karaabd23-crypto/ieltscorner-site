<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead">CELPIP Speaking Task 3 asks you to describe a photograph in detail. Precision in vocabulary and spatial language separates CLB 8 responses from CLB 9. This lesson teaches you to move from general to specific systematically.</p></div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Foreground to background</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Describe from the closest elements to the most distant</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"In the foreground... In the middle ground... In the background..."</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Specific nouns</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Replace "a person" with their visible role or identifying feature</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"A woman in a white lab coat" not "a person"</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Activity + manner</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Say what they are doing and how</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"A man is cycling quickly along a narrow path" not "a man is on a bike"</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Atmosphere phrase</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Add one phrase describing the mood or setting quality</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"The scene has a calm, early-morning quality" or "The setting appears to be a busy urban market."</p>
</div>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Vague location</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>There are people somewhere outside.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>A group of six adults is gathered around a picnic table in what appears to be a public park.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use spatial language (in the foreground, at the centre, to the left) and location descriptors (public park, indoor market).</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Only stating the obvious</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>A woman is standing. A man is sitting. There is a table.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>A woman in a dark blazer is leaning forward across a table, gesturing toward a document, while a man across from her listens attentively.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Add manner (how), posture, and visible relationship between people to move beyond surface description.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Single-tense monotony</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>The woman is standing. The man is sitting. The child is playing.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The woman stands at the front while two students remain seated at their desks, reviewing notes.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Mix simple present and present continuous. "Stands" and "remains" add variety alongside "is reviewing".</p>
</article>
</div>

## Why It Matters
<p class="lesson-importance">CELPIP Speaking Task 3 is assessed on the quality and precision of description, not just the quantity of words. Candidates who use spatial structure (foreground, background), specific nouns (role, clothing, object), and activity-manner combinations consistently score CLB 9 on Vocabulary and Coherence, while those who rely on "there are people doing things" remain at CLB 7-8.</p>
