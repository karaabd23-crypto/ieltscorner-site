## Goal
Use noun formation to increase lexical precision and cohesion in IELTS/CELPIP arguments.

## B2 Focus
At B2, noun formation should help you write more concisely and connect ideas across a paragraph.

Example:
- Wordy: *People feel unhappy because prices rise quickly.*
- Better: *Rapid price increases cause public dissatisfaction.*

## High-Value Noun Families
- regulate -> regulation -> deregulation
- employ -> employment -> unemployment
- invest -> investment
- educate -> education
- resist -> resistance
- sustain -> sustainability

## How Noun Formation Improves Writing
1. Builds formal style: *analyze the problem* -> *problem analysis*
2. Improves cohesion: repeat key noun family across sentences
3. Compresses ideas: fewer words, stronger precision

## Practice
### Exercise 1: Best Noun Choice
Choose the best noun.
1. The policy created major labor market ______. (disrupt / disruption / disruptive)
2. Urban ______ has outpaced infrastructure growth. (expand / expansion / expansive)
3. The proposal lacks financial ______. (sustain / sustainability / sustainable)

### Exercise 2: Rewrite for Precision
Rewrite each sentence using at least one formed noun.
1. The city is growing quickly and this causes transport problems.
2. People oppose the plan because they do not trust the process.
3. The economy improved after the government invested more.

### Exercise 3: IELTS/CELPIP Body Paragraph
Write one body paragraph (6 sentences):
*Should governments spend more on public transport than roads?*
Use at least 8 formed nouns.

## Answer Key
### Exercise 1
1. disruption 2. expansion 3. sustainability

### Exercise 2 (Possible)
1. *Rapid urban growth has caused transport congestion.*
2. *Public opposition increased due to low trust in the decision-making process.*
3. *Economic improvement followed increased government investment.*
