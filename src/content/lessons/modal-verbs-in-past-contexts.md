<div class="lesson-context">
  <p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Using Modal Verbs for Past Actions</strong> enables you to convey nuanced meanings about what might have been, what should have occurred, and what was possible in the past. Mastering these forms is essential for clear and precise communication in both written and spoken English.</p>
</div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>She could attended the meeting if she had known about it.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>She could have attended the meeting if she had known about it.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">'Could have' is used to express a past possibility that didn't happen.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>They should called before visiting.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>They should have called before visiting.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">'Should have' is used to express regret or criticism about a past action.</p>
</article>
</div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>Modal verbs in past contexts express nuances about past actions, such as missed opportunities or hypothetical situations. They often involve modal verbs like 'could have', 'should have', and 'would have'. At the B2 level, understanding these forms enhances your ability to convey precise meanings about past events.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Describing a past action that was possible but did not happen (e.g., 'could have').</li>
      <li>Expressing regret or criticism about a past action (e.g., 'should have').</li>
      <li>Discussing hypothetical situations in the past (e.g., 'would have').</li>
      <li>Reflecting on past obligations (e.g., 'must have').</li>
      <li>Speculating about past events (e.g., 'might have').</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">She <strong>could have attended</strong> the meeting if she had known about it.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">They <strong>should have called</strong> before visiting.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">He <strong>would have succeeded</strong> if he had tried harder.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Use 'could have' to talk about past possibilities that didn't occur.</li>
      <li>Use 'should have' for past actions that are regretted or criticized.</li>
      <li>Use 'would have' for hypothetical past situations.</li>
      <li>Always follow the modal with 'have' and a past participle.</li>
      <li>Avoid using 'to' after modal verbs in past contexts.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>omitting 'have' after a modal verb</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>He would finished the project on time if he had started earlier.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>He would have finished the project on time if he had started earlier.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Always use 'have' after a modal verb in past contexts.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>using 'to' after a modal verb</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>She might to have seen the movie already.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>She might have seen the movie already.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Do not use 'to' after modal verbs in past contexts.</p>
</article>
</div>
