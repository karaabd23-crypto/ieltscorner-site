<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Capitalization Rules</strong> are essential for maintaining clarity and professionalism in writing. Proper capitalization helps readers identify the beginning of sentences and recognize important names and titles. In this lesson, you will learn how to apply these rules effectively in your writing.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span> the president of the united states visited france.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span> The President of the United States visited France.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">Proper nouns and titles should always be capitalized.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span> she loves reading the book "to kill a mockingbird".</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span> She loves reading the book "To Kill a Mockingbird".</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">Titles of books should have major words capitalized.</p>
</article>
</div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>Capitalization involves using uppercase letters at the beginning of sentences and for proper nouns, titles, and other specific cases. It ensures clarity by marking sentence starts and emphasizing important words.</p>
    <p>At the B2 level, mastering capitalization is important for producing formal and polished writing.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Starting a new sentence to indicate its beginning.</li>
      <li>Writing proper nouns such as names of people, places, and organizations.</li>
      <li>Capitalizing major words in titles of books, movies, and other works.</li>
      <li>Using the pronoun "I" in any part of a sentence.</li>
      <li>Writing days of the week, months, and holidays to denote specificity.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence"><strong>London</strong> is a major city in <strong>England</strong>.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">She read <strong>"Pride and Prejudice"</strong> last month.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">Every <strong>Monday</strong>, I visit my grandmother.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Always capitalize the first word of a sentence.</li>
      <li>Capitalize all proper nouns and titles.</li>
      <li>Do not capitalize common nouns unless they start a sentence.</li>
      <li>Always capitalize "I" in any context.</li>
      <li>Be consistent with capitalization in titles and headings.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>Not capitalizing the first word of a sentence</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span> she went to the market.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span> She went to the market.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Always capitalize the first word of a sentence.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>Forgetting to capitalize proper nouns</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span> he traveled to paris in april.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span> He traveled to Paris in April.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Capitalize names of cities and months.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>Incorrect capitalization in titles</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span> she watched "the lord of the rings" last night.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span> She watched "The Lord of the Rings" last night.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Capitalize major words in titles.</p>
</article>
</div>
