<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead">CELPIP Speaking Task 7 asks for your opinion on a topic with some personal or societal relevance. CLB 9 responses give a clear position, one developed reason, and a concrete example or evidence. Surface-level opinions with no development score below CLB 8.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Weak</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>"I think it is a good idea because it has a lot of benefits and can help many people in different ways."</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">

  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>"I strongly support flexible work arrangements because they measurably reduce employee burnout. In a 2023 survey of Canadian tech workers, those with flexible hours reported 30% lower rates of stress-related absences compared to those on fixed schedules. That outcome alone makes the policy worth implementing."</p>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Position in sentence 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">State your opinion before giving any reasons</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"I firmly believe that mandatory language training benefits immigrant workers more than voluntary programs."</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">One developed reason</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Give one reason with an explanation, not three bare reasons</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"The primary reason is that mandatory enrollment removes the stigma associated with self-identifying as a language learner."</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Concrete example</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Add a specific example, statistic, or personal experience</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"In my own workplace, a colleague who completed a mandatory onboarding program was more willing to ask for clarification than those who self-enrolled."</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Closing restatement</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Restate your position in one sentence at the end</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"For these reasons, I believe mandatory programs produce stronger long-term outcomes than optional ones."</p>
</div>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Three reasons, no depth</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>It is good because it saves time, money, and helps people.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The primary reason I support this is the time saved: a 30-minute daily commute eliminated over a year saves approximately 130 hours -- equivalent to more than three full work weeks.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Pick one reason and develop it fully rather than listing three reasons at surface level.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">No example</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>It has many benefits for people in many different situations.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>For example, a nurse who works 12-hour shifts benefits more from flexible start times than from a salary increase, according to retention data from Ontario hospitals.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Every opinion benefit from a concrete example. Use "for example", "in my experience", or a specific data point.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Weak position signal</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>I think maybe it could be a good idea.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>I strongly support this policy for one specific reason.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Remove "maybe" from your position statement. Confidence in your stated opinion is scored under Task Fulfilment.</p>
</article>
</div>

## Why It Matters
<p class="lesson-importance">CELPIP Speaking Task 7 assesses depth of reasoning, not breadth of reasons. Candidates who list three or four benefits at surface level rarely score above CLB 8 on Coherence and Cohesion because there is no development chain to follow. One position, one developed reason with an explanation, and one concrete example is the CLB 9 structure -- and it takes approximately 45-55 seconds, which fits naturally within the task time.</p>
