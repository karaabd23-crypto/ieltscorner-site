## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Subject-verb agreement</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Singular subject needs singular verb; plural needs plural</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"The committee has agreed" not "have agreed"</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Consistent tense</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Do not shift tense mid-paragraph without a time signal</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"She arrived, sat down, and opened her laptop." — all simple past</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Article accuracy</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Use "a/an" for first mention, "the" for established referents</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"I saw a dog. The dog was barking."</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Preposition precision</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Memorise high-frequency preposition collocations</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"interested in" not "interested on"; "responsible for" not "responsible of"</p>
</div>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Tense shift</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>I went to the mall and I am buying a jacket.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>I went to the mall and bought a jacket.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Keep the same tense throughout a narrative unless a time shift is explicit.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Article error</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>I need the advice from my manager.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>I need advice from my manager.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Uncountable nouns like "advice" rarely take "the" unless the referent is already established.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Subject-verb mismatch</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>The group of employees are waiting.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The group of employees is waiting.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> The head noun is "group" (singular), not "employees".</p>
</article>
</div>

## Why It Matters
<p class="lesson-importance">CELPIP Linguistic Range and Accuracy at CLB 9 requires consistent control of complex grammar with only minor errors that do not impede communication. Candidates who reach CLB 9 on vocabulary but drop to CLB 7 on accuracy almost always make tense, article, or subject-verb errors that a 60-second grammar checklist would catch.</p>
