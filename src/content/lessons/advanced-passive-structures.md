<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Advanced Passive Voice</strong> shifts the focus from the subject to the action or result. This lesson helps you decide when to use passive voice for clarity and emphasis, and when active voice might be more appropriate.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>People recycle more plastic in this city every year.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>More plastic is recycled in this city every year.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The passive voice emphasizes the action of recycling rather than the people doing it.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>The committee will announce the results tomorrow.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The results will be announced tomorrow.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The passive voice is used to focus on the results rather than the committee.</p>
</article>
</div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>The <strong>passive voice</strong> emphasizes the action or its recipient rather than the subject performing the action. It is formed using a form of "be" followed by a past participle. At the B2 level, mastering this structure is crucial for emphasizing results or when the actor is unknown or irrelevant.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>You want to highlight the action or result rather than the actor.</li>
      <li>The actor is unknown or not important to the context.</li>
      <li>In formal writing, to maintain objectivity.</li>
      <li>In scientific or technical writing, where the process or result is more critical than the person doing it.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence"><strong>The report was completed</strong> by the team yesterday.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">The new policy <strong>will be implemented</strong> next month.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Use "be" + past participle to form the passive.</li>
      <li>Include "by" + agent only if necessary for clarity.</li>
      <li>Maintain the original tense when converting from active to passive.</li>
      <li>Switch to active voice if it clarifies responsibility.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>using an active verb form where passive focus is required</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The final report publish next Monday.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The final report will be published next Monday.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use "be" + past participle to correctly form the passive voice.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>forgetting the auxiliary verb in a passive sentence</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The final decision announced yesterday.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The final decision was announced yesterday.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Ensure to include a form of "be" with the past participle.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>using passive voice when the active sentence is clearer</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Mistakes were made by the planning team during the launch.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The planning team made mistakes during the launch.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use active voice to clarify responsibility when it's important.</p>
</article>
</div>
