## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Too weak</span>The speaker brought the cost issue in the meeting.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The speaker brought up the cost issue in the meeting.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">"Brought up" correctly introduces the topic, making the sentence clearer.</p>

  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>Please look up the station name before you leave.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">"Look up" is used correctly, improving the sentence's naturalness.</p>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>Phrasal verbs are combinations of verbs and particles that create a meaning different from the individual words. In the context of work, they help convey precise actions or ideas succinctly.</p>
    <p>For example, "bring up" means to introduce a topic. Mastering these at a B2 level helps in achieving fluency and naturalness in professional communication.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Discussing topics in meetings or presentations (e.g., "bring up").</li>
      <li>Searching for information (e.g., "look up").</li>
      <li>Executing tasks or plans (e.g., "carry out").</li>
      <li>Ensuring clarity and naturalness in professional emails or reports.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">The manager <strong>brought up</strong> the new policy during the meeting.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">You should <strong>look up</strong> the client information before the call.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">The team <strong>carried out</strong> the project successfully.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Always learn phrasal verbs as a complete unit.</li>
      <li>Check if the object can be placed between the verb and particle.</li>
      <li>Use phrasal verbs to make your language more natural and precise.</li>
      <li>Be aware of the context and register to ensure appropriateness.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>choosing the wrong particle with a familiar verb</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The manager pointed the delay out about at the meeting.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The manager pointed out the delay at the meeting.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> learn the full phrasal verb as one item</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>placing the object in an unnatural position</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Please look the timetable up before class.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Please look up the timetable before class.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> check where the object belongs in the phrase</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>using a phrasal verb without understanding its meaning</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The team carried out late because of traffic.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The team carried out the safety check despite the traffic delay.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> use the phrasal verb only when you know what action it describes</p>
</article>
</div>
