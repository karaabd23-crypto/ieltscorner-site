<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Collective Nouns</strong> help you ensure subject-verb agreement in sentences where the subject refers to a group acting as a single entity or as individuals. Mastering this concept is key to maintaining clarity and precision in your writing, especially at the B2 level.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>The team are winning the match.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The team is winning the match.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">'Team' acts as a single entity here, so a singular verb is needed.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>The audience was clapping their hands.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The audience were clapping their hands.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">'Audience' acts as individuals, so a plural verb is appropriate.</p>
</article>
</div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p><strong>Collective nouns</strong> refer to groups of people or things considered as a single entity, such as 'team', 'family', or 'audience'. They require careful subject-verb agreement, as they can take either a singular or plural verb depending on whether the group acts as a single unit or as individuals. Mastering this distinction is crucial at the B2 level for achieving clarity and precision in writing.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Describing a group acting as a single entity (e.g., 'The team <strong>is</strong> winning.').</li>
      <li>Describing individual actions within a group (e.g., 'The team <strong>are</strong> arguing among themselves.').</li>
      <li>Writing formal reports or essays where precision is key.</li>
      <li>Communicating in professional settings where grammatical accuracy is expected.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">The committee <strong>has</strong> decided on the new policy.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">The committee <strong>are</strong> having lunch at their desks.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Use a singular verb when the group acts as a single unit.</li>
      <li>Use a plural verb when individuals within the group act separately.</li>
      <li>In formal writing, prefer singular verbs unless context clearly indicates individual actions.</li>
      <li>Be consistent with verb agreement throughout your writing.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>Using a singular verb for a group acting individually</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The staff is taking their breaks at different times.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The staff are taking their breaks at different times.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use a plural verb when the group members act individually.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>Using a plural verb for a group acting as a single unit</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The jury have reached a verdict.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The jury has reached a verdict.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use a singular verb when the group acts as one unit.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>Inconsistent verb agreement with collective nouns</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The class has finished their assignments and are leaving.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The class has finished its assignments and is leaving.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Maintain consistent verb agreement throughout the sentence.</p>
</article>
</div>
