<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Essay Structure: Introduction</strong> is crucial for setting the tone and direction of your essay. A strong introduction is concise, clearly states the essay's purpose, and seamlessly connects to the body paragraphs. This lesson will guide you on what to include and avoid to craft effective essay introductions.</p></div>

<div class="lesson-example-grid">

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>An essay introduction serves to orient the reader to the topic and provide a clear thesis statement. It sets the stage for the arguments that will follow in the body paragraphs. A well-crafted introduction is concise and directly connected to the essay's main points.</p>
    <p>The introduction should paraphrase the essay topic, provide context, and include a thesis statement that outlines the essay's direction. At the B2 level, mastering this structure enhances clarity and coherence, essential for higher scores.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Writing essays for exams like IELTS and CELPIP.</li>
      <li>Introducing a topic in academic or formal writing.</li>
      <li>Establishing the main argument or purpose of your essay.</li>
      <li>Connecting the introduction to the body paragraphs effectively.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence"><strong>The rapid growth of technology</strong> has significantly altered modern communication methods.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence"><strong>This essay examines the impact of technology</strong> on communication, arguing that its benefits outweigh the drawbacks.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Start with a paraphrase of the topic to set context.</li>
      <li>Avoid broad or dictionary-style definitions.</li>
      <li>Include a clear thesis statement outlining your main argument.</li>
      <li>Ensure the thesis aligns with the body paragraphs.</li>
      <li>Keep the introduction concise and focused.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>starting the introduction with a dictionary definition</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Economics is defined as the study of how people use resources.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Economics plays a crucial role in shaping national policies and global trade.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Begin with an engaging statement about the topic's relevance.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>failing to state your position clearly in the introduction</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>There are many opinions on this issue.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Despite differing views, this essay argues that renewable energy is vital for future sustainability.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Clearly state your position in the thesis statement.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>making the introduction too long by explaining all arguments</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>First, I will discuss the environment. Then, I will discuss the economy. Finally, I will discuss society...</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>This essay will examine the environmental, economic, and social impacts of renewable energy.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Provide a concise thesis and reserve details for the body paragraphs.</p>
</article>
</div>
