<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Noun Phrases</strong> are essential for constructing detailed and precise sentences. They allow you to convey complex ideas succinctly, especially in formal and academic writing. Mastering noun phrases ensures your writing remains clear and effective, enhancing both comprehension and impact.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>The city launched a policy transport new plan.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The city launched a new public transport plan.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The improved sentence correctly orders the modifiers to clearly describe the noun.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>The report discussed housing affordable long-term options.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The report discussed long-term affordable housing options.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">This version places modifiers in a logical order, enhancing clarity.</p>
</article>
</div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>A <strong>noun phrase</strong> is a group of words that includes a noun and provides more information about it. It functions as a single unit in a sentence, often serving as the subject, object, or complement. Noun phrases add detail and specificity, making sentences more informative and precise. At the B2 level, mastering noun phrases helps in constructing complex and nuanced sentences, essential for academic and formal writing.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Describing a subject or object in detail in academic essays.</li>
      <li>Writing reports that require precision and clarity.</li>
      <li>Creating complex sentences in formal communication.</li>
      <li>Summarizing information succinctly in presentations or articles.</li>
      <li>Conveying specific information in business documents.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">The <strong>new public transport plan</strong> was well received by the community.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">She presented a <strong>comprehensive market analysis report</strong> during the meeting.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Identify the head noun and ensure it remains clear.</li>
      <li>Place modifiers in a logical order before the noun.</li>
      <li>Avoid overloading the noun phrase with too many modifiers.</li>
      <li>Convert part of the phrase into a clause if clarity is lost.</li>
      <li>Ensure the noun phrase conveys a single, clear idea.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>stacking modifiers in an order that makes the noun phrase hard to read</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The school announced a support reading after-class program.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The school announced an after-class reading support program.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Arrange modifiers logically to maintain clarity of the head noun.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>hiding the head noun under too many modifiers</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The report recommended a community support transport emergency plan.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The report recommended an emergency community transport support plan.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Ensure the head noun is prominent and modifiers are logically ordered.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>using a noun phrase where a full clause would be clearer</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The rapid district late-bus reduction target created confusion.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The district target to reduce late buses quickly created confusion.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use a clause to enhance clarity when the noun phrase becomes cumbersome.</p>
</article>
</div>
