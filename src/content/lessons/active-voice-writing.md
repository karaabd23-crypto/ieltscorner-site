<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Active Voice in Writing</strong> is a writing skill crucial for IELTS and CELPIP. This lesson focuses on how using active voice can enhance clarity and directness in your responses, ensuring that your writing is both effective and engaging.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Too weak</span>The report was written by the manager and it was sent to the team.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The manager wrote the report and sent it to the team.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The stronger sentence uses active voice to clearly show who performed the actions.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Too weak</span>Errors are found, they are reported, and then they are fixed by staff.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>Staff find errors, report them, and fix them the same day.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The better version uses active voice to streamline the sentence and clarify the actions.</p>
</article>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>Active voice is a grammatical structure where the subject performs the action expressed by the verb. It enhances clarity and directness by clearly indicating who is responsible for the action. In active voice, sentences typically follow the pattern: subject + verb + object. This structure is vital at the B2 level for conveying ideas clearly and effectively, particularly in exam writing contexts.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Writing task responses in IELTS and CELPIP to demonstrate clarity and control.</li>
      <li>Describing processes or actions where identifying the doer is crucial.</li>
      <li>Strengthening sentence impact and directness in academic or formal writing.</li>
      <li>Editing drafts to improve readability and engagement.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence"><strong>The manager</strong> wrote the report and sent it to the team.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence"><strong>Staff</strong> find errors, report them, and fix them the same day.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Use active voice to make sentences clearer and more direct.</li>
      <li>Place the subject before the verb to emphasize the doer.</li>
      <li>Avoid passive voice when the doer is known and relevant.</li>
      <li>Active voice is often more engaging and easier to understand.</li>
      <li>In exams, active voice can help demonstrate control and clarity.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>using passive voice when the doer is clear and important</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The report was written by the manager and it was sent to the team.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The manager wrote the report and sent it to the team.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> If you know who does the action and it matters, put the doer first.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>stacking multiple passive verbs in one sentence</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Errors are found, they are reported, and then they are fixed by staff.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Staff find errors, report them, and fix them the same day.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Rebuild the sentence with one clear subject doing all the actions.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>choosing passive voice to sound formal and ending up vague</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Improvements have been made and results have been achieved.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The team improved the process and reduced errors by 30%.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Add a real subject and a measurable result. specificity reads as confident, not casual.</p>
</article>
</div>
