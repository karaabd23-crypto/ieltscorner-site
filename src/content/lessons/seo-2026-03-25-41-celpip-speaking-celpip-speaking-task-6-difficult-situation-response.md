<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead">CELPIP Speaking Task 6 presents a problem situation and asks how you would respond. CLB 9 responses identify the core issue, state a response strategy, and show interpersonal awareness. Vague reassurances score below CLB 8.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Weak</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>"I would just try to talk to them and hopefully the problem would go away eventually."</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Stronger</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>"I would request a private meeting with my colleague to address the miscommunication directly. ## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Identify the core issue</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Name the specific problem before proposing a response</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"The core issue is that the miscommunication created conflicting expectations about who owned the deliverable."</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Step-by-step response</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Give two or three ordered steps, not a single vague action</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"First, I would request a meeting. Then, I would present the timeline. Finally, I would agree on a revised deadline."</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Interpersonal awareness</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">Acknowledge the other person's perspective or feelings</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"I understand my colleague may feel frustrated, so I would choose a private setting to avoid public embarrassment."</p>
</div>
<div class="lesson-teach-panel">
  <p class="lesson-teach-pattern">Resolution clause</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-meaning">End with what the situation will look like after your response</p>
  <p class="lesson-teach-example">"The goal is to leave the meeting with a written agreement on roles so this does not repeat."</p>
</div>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Single vague action</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>I would just talk to them and fix the problem.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>I would schedule a 15-minute one-on-one, share specific examples of the conflict, and agree on a protocol for future handoffs.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> A specific, sequenced response with concrete details scores CLB 9. "Talk to them" does not.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">No acknowledgement of the other person</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>I would tell them what they did wrong and what they need to fix.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>I would invite them to share their perspective before explaining mine, to ensure I have the full picture.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Task 6 scenarios often involve interpersonal conflict. Showing awareness of the other party's experience is scored under Coherence.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">No resolution clause</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Avoid</span>I would handle the situation and move on.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The resolution is a written agreement on roles that both parties sign, so there is a clear reference point for future conflicts.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> A concrete resolution shows the examiner your response is complete, not just started.</p>
</article>
</div>

## Why It Matters
<p class="lesson-importance">CELPIP Speaking Task 6 is an interpersonal problem-solving task. The examiner is looking for situational awareness, a structured response, and a clear resolution goal. Candidates who give one vague action ("I would just talk to them") score CLB 7 on Task Fulfilment. A three-part response (core issue, ordered steps, resolution goal) with interpersonal awareness reliably achieves CLB 9.</p>
