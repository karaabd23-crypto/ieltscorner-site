<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>IELTS Writing Task 1: Maps and Comparisons</strong> involves describing significant changes on a map over time or between different layouts. The key is to highlight the most important transformations and organize details logically, making it easier for the reader to understand. This lesson will guide you in writing an effective overview and structuring your report for clarity.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Too weak</span>There are many changes on the map and many things are different.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span><strong>Overall</strong>, the area became more residential, while several industrial features were removed or relocated.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The stronger sentence gives the paragraph a clear direction by summarizing the major changes.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Too weak</span>On the left there is something and on the right there is something else.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span><strong>In the northern section</strong>, the old factory was replaced by a housing complex and a small parking area.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The better version develops the idea by specifying the changes and their locations.</p>
</article>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>In IELTS Writing Task 1, map reports require you to describe changes over time or between different layouts. The goal is to highlight significant transformations and organize information logically.</p>
    <p>Effective map reports group changes, emphasize major transformations, and use precise location language. The overview should summarize the main changes rather than list every detail.</p>
    <p>For B2 level students, mastering paragraph structure and coherence is key to stronger writing, rather than simply using more complex language.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Describing changes in a map over time for IELTS Writing Task 1.</li>
      <li>Writing an overview that highlights major transformations.</li>
      <li>Grouping changes by area or type to enhance clarity.</li>
      <li>Using specific language to describe location and changes.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence"><strong>Overall</strong>, the area became more residential, while several industrial features were removed or relocated.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence"><strong>In the northern section</strong>, the old factory was replaced by a housing complex and a small parking area.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Identify the two or three biggest overall changes on the map.</li>
      <li>Write the overview before detailing specific changes.</li>
      <li>Group details logically by area or transformation type.</li>
      <li>Use comparison language such as "was replaced by," "expanded," and "converted into."</li>
      <li>Ensure location phrases are clear for accurate visualization.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>Listing small details before giving an overview</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>There is a road, a tree area, a car park, and a building on the map.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span><strong>Overall</strong>, the town shifted toward residential use, with industrial spaces reduced and new housing introduced.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Write the big-picture overview before the smaller details.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>Moving around the map randomly</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The school changed, then the river, then the center, then the northern road.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span><strong>In the northern area</strong>, the former warehouse site was converted into apartments connected by a new access road.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Group details by area or by type of change.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>Adding opinion to a report task</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>This was a very good development for the town.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The old industrial area was converted into a residential zone with new access roads.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Describe what changed. Do not evaluate it.</p>
</article>
</div>
