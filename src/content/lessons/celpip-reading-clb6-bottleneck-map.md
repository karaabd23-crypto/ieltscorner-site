## Intro
Many CELPIP students have CLB 8+ in speaking or listening but remain at CLB 6 in reading. This lesson maps the main causes so you can stop practicing randomly.

## Examiner Criteria
- Correct answer selection based on textual evidence, not keyword familiarity.
- Stable accuracy across question types under time pressure.
- Consistent comprehension of purpose, viewpoint, and detail.

## Mistakes
- Treating all question types with one reading method.
- Matching words without verifying meaning in context.
- Spending too long on one hard question and rushing the end.

## Strategy
1. Track your errors by question type for three timed sets.
2. Mark each error as one of four causes: distractor, inference, viewpoint, or timing.
3. Build practice blocks that target only your top two causes.

## Example
Weak approach: "I got many wrong because the section was hard."

Better approach: "Most misses came from inference and viewpoint items in Part 4, so I need proof-based inference drills."

## Exercise
1. Review your last reading set and classify each wrong answer by cause.
2. Count which cause appears most often.
3. Plan one 30-minute practice block for that cause only.
