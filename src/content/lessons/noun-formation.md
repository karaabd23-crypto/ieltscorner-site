<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Noun formation</strong> helps you build clearer noun ideas. Sometimes English needs a noun form, and sometimes the noun group becomes too long or too heavy. In this lesson, you will learn how to keep noun forms accurate and readable for effective communication.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>The committee will decide next week, and this decide may affect funding.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The committee will decide next week, and this decision may affect funding.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">'Decision' is the correct noun form of 'decide' and fits naturally in the sentence.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>The company wants to expand, but the expand will take two years.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The company wants to expand, but the expansion will take two years.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">'Expansion' is the noun form of 'expand', making the sentence clearer.</p>
</article>
</div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>Noun formation involves creating nouns from verbs or adjectives using suffixes such as -tion, -ment, -ness, and -ity. This process helps in naming actions, results, and abstract ideas more clearly and is essential for precise communication, especially at the B2 level where complex ideas need to be expressed succinctly.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>You need a noun after a determiner or preposition in a sentence.</li>
      <li>Converting verbs or adjectives to nouns for clarity in reports and essays.</li>
      <li>Avoiding overly complex noun phrases in formal writing.</li>
      <li>Describing trends, causes, and outcomes in academic or exam settings.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">The <strong>decision</strong> to cut costs improved the company's financial health.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">Her <strong>happiness</strong> was evident during the celebration.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Use noun forms after determiners, possessives, and prepositions.</li>
      <li>Choose the correct suffix to convert verbs or adjectives into nouns.</li>
      <li>Ensure noun phrases are concise and clear; avoid long chains.</li>
      <li>Check for correct article usage with the new noun form.</li>
      <li>Rephrase if the sentence becomes too abstract or dense.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>Using a verb form where a noun is required</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Their investigate into the accident took three months.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Their investigation into the accident took three months.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use noun forms after determiners and prepositions.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>Leaving an adjective form where a noun is needed</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The policy produced stable in small towns.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The policy produced stability in small towns.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Convert adjectives to nouns for correct sentence structure.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>Creating unreadable noun chains</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The council discussed transport improve plan funding.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The council discussed funding for a transport improvement plan.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Simplify noun phrases by using prepositional phrases.</p>
</article>
</div>
