<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Suffixes</strong> are letters added to the end of a word to change its grammatical function, such as converting verbs to nouns or adjectives to adverbs. Mastering suffixes allows you to express ideas more precisely, which is essential for effective communication at a B2 level.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>The city needs a more effect response to late buses.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The city needs a more effective response to late buses.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The suffix '-ive' is used to form an adjective that describes the type of response needed.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>Regular speaking practice improved pronounce and confident.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>Regular speaking practice improved pronunciation and confidence.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The suffix '-ation' turns 'pronounce' into 'pronunciation', a noun, and '-ence' turns 'confident' into 'confidence'.</p>
</article>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>Suffixes are letters added to the end of a base word to alter its grammatical function, such as changing a verb to a noun or an adjective to an adverb. They help create more precise expressions by modifying the word class.</p>
    <p>For example, adding '-tion' to 'inform' creates the noun 'information'. At the B2 level, mastering suffixes is crucial for nuanced communication.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Converting verbs to nouns for formal writing (e.g., 'decide' to 'decision').</li>
      <li>Changing adjectives to adverbs in descriptive writing (e.g., 'quick' to 'quickly').</li>
      <li>Creating adjectives from nouns for detailed descriptions (e.g., 'help' to 'helpful').</li>
      <li>Forming nouns from adjectives to discuss abstract concepts (e.g., 'happy' to 'happiness').</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">The <strong>decision</strong> was made after careful consideration.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">She reacted <strong>quickly</strong> to the unexpected news.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Identify the required word class before adding a suffix.</li>
      <li>Use common suffixes like '-tion', '-ment', '-ness', '-able', '-ive', and '-ly'.</li>
      <li>Adjust spelling when necessary (e.g., 'happy' becomes 'happiness').</li>
      <li>Ensure the new word fits naturally within the sentence context.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>using a suffix that does not match the sentence slot</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The new policy brought stable to the bus schedule.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The new policy brought stability to the bus schedule.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use the noun-forming suffix '-ity' to match the required noun slot.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>using a base adjective where the sentence needs a noun</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The campaign increased aware of road safety.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The campaign increased awareness of road safety.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use the noun-forming suffix '-ness' for the noun slot.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>using an adjective where a verb modifier needs an adverb suffix</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The driver reacted quick when the signal changed.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The driver reacted quickly when the signal changed.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Add '-ly' to form an adverb that modifies the verb.</p>
</article>
</div>
