<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>CELPIP Writing Task 2: Survey Response</strong> requires you to choose an option and justify it with clear, logical reasons. A successful response is direct, well-supported, and easy to follow. This lesson will guide you in making a clear choice, supporting it effectively, and concluding with impact, ensuring your response is persuasive and concise.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Too weak</span>This topic has good points and bad points in many ways.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>I would choose the public transport plan because it helps more residents on a daily basis.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The stronger sentence provides a clear and direct choice, guiding the reader effectively.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Too weak</span>Both choices are important and it depends on many things.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>I would select the new recycling program because it significantly reduces waste and promotes environmental sustainability.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The improved sentence clearly states a choice and provides a specific benefit.</p>
</article>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>In CELPIP Writing Task 2: Survey Response, you must select one option from a given survey and justify your choice with clear, specific reasons. This task involves presenting a structured argument: state your choice, support it with two distinct reasons, and provide a practical example or consequence. At the B2 level, mastering this structure demonstrates your ability to communicate clearly and logically in writing.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Responding to a CELPIP survey task that requires a clear choice.</li>
      <li>Explaining your decision in a concise and logical manner.</li>
      <li>Providing real-life examples to support your argument.</li>
      <li>Contrasting your choice with another option to strengthen your argument.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence"><strong>I would choose</strong> the public transport plan <strong>because it helps more residents on a daily basis.</strong></div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence"><strong>In addition,</strong> the benefits are long-term: <strong>lower congestion can improve punctuality for workers and students.</strong></div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Always choose one option and state it clearly at the beginning.</li>
      <li>Support your choice with two distinct reasons.</li>
      <li>Include a practical example or consequence to strengthen your argument.</li>
      <li>Only mention the other option if it helps contrast your main point.</li>
      <li>Conclude with a sentence that reaffirms your choice.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>Delaying the choice</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Both choices are useful and there are many things to discuss first.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>I would choose the public transport option because it serves a larger number of daily commuters.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> State your choice in the first sentence to set a clear direction.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>Repetition without substance</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>It is better because it is more useful, more helpful, and more beneficial.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>It is better because it serves more residents and reduces daily commuting stress.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Provide two distinct reasons rather than repeating the same idea.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>Lacking practical examples</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>This is why I think it is the better option.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>For example, better transit can help workers arrive on time and lower transport costs over time.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Include a practical example or consequence to support your choice.</p>
</article>
</div>
