## Intro
CELPIP reading questions switch between detail retrieval and main-meaning interpretation. CLB 6 readers often use the wrong reading mode for the question type.

## Examiner Criteria
- Detail questions are answered with precise evidence.
- Main-idea questions are answered from paragraph purpose and overall message.
- Candidate avoids over-reading for simple detail items.

## Mistakes
- Reading the whole passage deeply for a detail question.
- Choosing a detail answer for a main-purpose question.
- Missing scope words like "mainly", "best", or "overall".

## Strategy
1. Identify question mode first: Detail or General.
2. For Detail mode, scan target lines and verify exact wording.
3. For General mode, summarize paragraph purpose before choosing.

## Example
Question: "What is the main purpose of paragraph 3?"
Wrong approach: Choose a sentence-level detail.
Better approach: Summarize paragraph function, then pick the option that matches the full purpose.

## Exercise
1. Take six reading questions and tag each as Detail or General before solving.
2. Write one-line justification for your answer mode.
3. Review whether wrong answers came from using the wrong mode.
