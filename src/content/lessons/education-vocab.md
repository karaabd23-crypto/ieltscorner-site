## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>using broad school words instead of the exact education term</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The school helped weak students.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The school provided instructional support for students who were behind.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> replace broad verbs like help with the exact school action</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>listing school vocabulary without a real context</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Curriculum, assessment, attendance, support.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Regular assessment improved attendance because students received earlier support.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> place each new word in a sentence about a real school situation</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>using a topic word with the wrong partner phrase</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The school increased attendance lessons.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The school introduced support lessons that improved attendance.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> learn the topic word with a natural partner phrase</p>
</article>
</div>
