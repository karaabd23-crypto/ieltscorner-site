## Intro
Distractors are the main reason many students stay below CLB 8 in CELPIP reading. This lesson teaches a proof-first elimination system.

## Examiner Criteria
- Answer must match passage meaning, not only matching vocabulary.
- Incorrect options are rejected with clear textual justification.
- Final choice remains stable during review.

## Mistakes
- Choosing the first option that shares a keyword.
- Ignoring qualifiers like "mostly", "only", or "likely".
- Switching from a proven answer to a less-supported answer during review.

## Strategy
1. Locate the answer zone with keywords.
2. Read one sentence before and after the evidence line.
3. Eliminate options that overstate, narrow, or reverse the meaning.

## Example
Passage meaning: "Most respondents preferred option B after trying both options."

Distractor: "All respondents preferred option B."

Correct logic: "Most" does not equal "all", so the distractor is wrong.

## Exercise
1. Take five CELPIP multiple-choice questions.
2. For each question, write one sentence explaining why each wrong option is wrong.
3. Keep only answers that have direct evidence support.
