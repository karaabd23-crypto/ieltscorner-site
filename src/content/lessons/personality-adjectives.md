## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>Describing personality traits involves using specific adjectives to accurately characterize individuals. This vocabulary helps convey the nuances of someone's character, making descriptions more vivid and precise.</p>
    <p>At the B2 level, mastering these adjectives enhances both spoken and written communication by providing clarity and depth.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Writing character descriptions in essays or reports.</li>
      <li>Engaging in conversations about people’s behaviors and attitudes.</li>
      <li>Preparing for interviews or discussions that require personal insights.</li>
      <li>Describing characters in stories or narratives.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">She is <strong>meticulous</strong>, ensuring every detail is perfect.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">His <strong>charismatic</strong> nature draws people to him effortlessly.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Choose adjectives that precisely match the personality trait you want to describe.</li>
      <li>Pair adjectives with examples to illustrate the trait in action.</li>
      <li>Avoid vague adjectives like "nice"; opt for more specific terms like "considerate" or "thoughtful."</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>using vague adjectives instead of specific traits</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>He is nice.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>He is <strong>compassionate</strong> and always willing to help others.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> choose adjectives that precisely describe the personality trait.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>using generic terms without context</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>She is friendly.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>She is <strong>approachable</strong>, making everyone feel welcome.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> provide context to illustrate the trait.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 3</p>
  <h3>mixing personality traits with unrelated terms</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>He is adaptable and listens actively.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>He is <strong>adaptable</strong>, and his <strong>active listening</strong> skills enhance team dynamics.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> ensure each term clearly fits the context of personality traits.</p>
</article>
</div>
