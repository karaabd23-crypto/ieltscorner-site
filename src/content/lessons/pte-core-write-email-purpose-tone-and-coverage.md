Write Email is a practical communication task. You need to understand who you are writing to, why you are writing, and what details must be included. A good email does not need rare vocabulary. It needs a clear purpose, organized details, and a tone that fits the situation.

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Main idea</p>
  <p>Use a simple email frame: greeting, purpose, details, request or action, closing. This frame works for requests, complaints, apologies, and information emails.</p>
  <p>Prompt coverage is essential. If the task asks for schedule, cost, and registration, your email should clearly mention all three. Fluency cannot fully repair a missing requirement.</p>
  <p>Tone depends on the reader. To a service office, school, manager, or community centre, use polite neutral language. To a friend, you can sound warmer, but the answer still needs structure.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Score-safe habits</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Open with the reason for writing.</li>
      <li>Turn each prompt bullet into a message block.</li>
      <li>Use polite request language when asking for information.</li>
      <li>End with a closing that matches the relationship.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Missing details</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Please send me information about classes.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Could you please send me the class schedule, fees, and registration steps?</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Cover every required point.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Too direct</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Send the price today.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>I would appreciate it if you could send the price information today.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use polite request language.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Unclear purpose</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>I saw your centre and have some questions.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>I am writing to ask about your evening English classes.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> State the purpose early.</p>
</article>

## Why It Matters
PTE Core is scored through computer-delivered tasks, but the human skill underneath is still clear communication. When you understand Write Email task control, you waste less time guessing what the task wants. You can focus on meaning, structure, grammar, pronunciation, or listening accuracy in the exact way the task requires. This makes practice more efficient and makes the simulator feel less intimidating.
