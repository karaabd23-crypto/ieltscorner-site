<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Using Parallelism for Emphasis in Writing</strong> enhances clarity and impact by aligning similar grammatical structures. This technique highlights key ideas and ensures your message is both clear and emphatic. This lesson will guide you in crafting sentences that are balanced and persuasive.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>She likes to swim, jogging, and biking.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>She likes <strong>swimming</strong>, <strong>jogging</strong>, and <strong>biking</strong>.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">This correction ensures all activities are in the gerund form, creating parallel structure.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>The manager was responsible for overseeing the project, to conduct meetings, and report progress.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>The manager was responsible for <strong>overseeing the project</strong>, <strong>conducting meetings</strong>, and <strong>reporting progress</strong>.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">This version uses parallel structure to list responsibilities clearly.</p>
</article>
</div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>Parallelism in writing involves using similar grammatical structures to create rhythm and enhance clarity. It helps in emphasizing ideas by making sentences more memorable and easier to read. This technique is crucial for B2 level learners to improve coherence and impact in their writing.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Listing items or actions in a sentence to ensure clarity.</li>
      <li>Creating comparisons or contrasts to highlight differences or similarities.</li>
      <li>Writing persuasive texts where emphasis on key points is needed.</li>
      <li>Constructing complex sentences where maintaining balance is essential.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">She enjoys <strong>reading</strong>, <strong>writing</strong>, and <strong>jogging</strong> in the morning.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">The project requires <strong>time</strong>, <strong>effort</strong>, and <strong>patience</strong>.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Ensure all elements in a list use the same grammatical form.</li>
      <li>Use parallelism to enhance readability and flow.</li>
      <li>Check that comparisons are balanced and clear.</li>
      <li>Avoid mixing different structures within the same sentence.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>Mixing forms in a list</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>He likes to swim, running, and to bike.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>He likes <strong>swimming</strong>, <strong>running</strong>, and <strong>biking</strong>.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use the same grammatical form for all items in a list.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>Inconsistent structure in comparisons</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>She is more interested in reading than to write.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>She is more interested in <strong>reading</strong> than <strong>writing</strong>.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Ensure both parts of a comparison use the same form.</p>
</article>
</div>
