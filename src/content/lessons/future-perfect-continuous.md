<div class="lesson-context"><p class="lesson-context-lead"><strong>Future Perfect Continuous</strong> helps you express ongoing actions that will continue up to a future point. Mastering this tense allows you to clearly communicate duration and progress in future contexts, enhancing both your writing and speaking skills.</p></div>

## Examples
<div class="lesson-example-grid">
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 1</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>By next year, she works here for ten years.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>By next year, she <strong>will have been working</strong> here for ten years.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">The Future Perfect Continuous tense emphasizes the duration of her work up to next year.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-example-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Example 2</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Incorrect</span>He will be tired because he runs all morning.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Better</span>He will be tired because he <strong>will have been running</strong> all morning.</p>
  <p class="lesson-card-note">This version correctly uses the Future Perfect Continuous to show the ongoing action leading to a future point.</p>
</article>
</div>

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Meaning</p>
    <p>The <strong>Future Perfect Continuous</strong> tense describes actions that will have been ongoing up to a specific point in the future. It highlights the duration of an activity. Formed with 'will have been' + present participle (verb + -ing), it is crucial at the B2 level for expressing complex future timelines.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-when">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Use it when</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Describing an action that will continue up to a future point in time.</li>
      <li>Emphasizing the duration of an activity before a specified future time.</li>
      <li>In future projections or plans where the duration is relevant.</li>
      <li>In formal writing to express future actions with precision.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-pattern">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">See it</p>
    <div class="lesson-pattern-stack">
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">By next year, <strong>she will have been working</strong> at the company for a decade.</div>
      <div class="lesson-pattern-sentence">When he retires, <strong>he will have been teaching</strong> for over 40 years.</div>
    </div>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Quick rules</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Use 'will have been' + verb + -ing to form the tense.</li>
      <li>Always specify a future time point to anchor the action.</li>
      <li>Emphasize the duration leading up to the future time.</li>
      <li>Avoid using this tense for actions that do not emphasize duration.</li>
      <li>Check that the future time point is clear in your sentence.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 1</p>
  <h3>Using simple future instead of Future Perfect Continuous</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>By 5 PM, they will finish the project.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>By 5 PM, they <strong>will have been finishing</strong> the project.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Use Future Perfect Continuous to emphasize the duration of the project work up to 5 PM.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Common problem 2</p>
  <h3>Forgetting to specify the future time point</h3>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>She will have been studying without a break.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>By the end of the day, she <strong>will have been studying</strong> without a break.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Always include a future time point to clarify the duration.</p>
</article>
</div>
