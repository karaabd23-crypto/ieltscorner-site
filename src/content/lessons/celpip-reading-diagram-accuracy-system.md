## Intro
Diagram tasks often look simple but reduce scores through avoidable detail mistakes. This lesson gives a repeatable accuracy system.

## Examiner Criteria
- Correct interpretation of labels, units, and directional clues.
- Accurate transfer of diagram information into answer choices.
- Efficient processing under time pressure.

## Mistakes
- Skimming instructions and missing what the question actually asks.
- Ignoring units, arrows, or category labels.
- Over-reading one visual detail and missing overall relationships.

## Strategy
1. Read the instruction line first and identify the required output.
2. Scan labels and units before reading answer options.
3. Confirm direction and relationship before selecting.

## Example
Instruction: "Choose the step that occurs immediately after packaging."
Diagram shows sequence: Sorting -> Assembly -> Packaging -> Inspection.

Correct answer: Inspection.

## Exercise
1. Take two diagram tasks and write the instruction in your own words.
2. List all units or labels before answering.
3. Answer only after confirming sequence or location.
