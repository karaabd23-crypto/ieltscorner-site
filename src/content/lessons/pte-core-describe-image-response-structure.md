Describe Image is not a race to mention everything. It is a short spoken report. You need to identify what the image shows, select the most important features, and speak in a clear order. A simple structure is more reliable than a long list of disconnected details.

## How It Works
<div class="lesson-teach-grid">
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-core">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Main idea</p>
  <p>Start with the type and topic: the chart shows, the map compares, the image illustrates. This gives the listener a frame immediately.</p>
  <p>Next, describe the main pattern. Is something increasing, decreasing, stable, different, or concentrated in one area? The main pattern is usually more important than small details.</p>
  <p>Finally, add one specific detail and a closing sentence. The closing does not need to be creative. It should simply summarize the most important meaning.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="lesson-panel lesson-panel-remember">
    <p class="lesson-panel-label">Score-safe habits</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Name the image type and topic first.</li>
      <li>Choose the biggest pattern before small details.</li>
      <li>Use comparison language when the image has groups.</li>
      <li>Finish with a clean overall statement.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

## Common Mistakes
<div class="lesson-error-grid">
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Listing without structure</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>Blue, red, 2020, 2021, high, low.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>The chart compares two groups and shows stronger growth in the blue group after 2021.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Group details into meaning.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Ignoring the overview</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>The first number is 15 and the second number is 18.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>Overall, the numbers increase over the period.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Start with the big picture.</p>
</article>
<article class="lesson-error-card">
  <p class="lesson-card-label">Overexplaining</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-weak"><span>Weak</span>I try to mention every label and run out of time.</p>
  <p class="lesson-line lesson-line-strong"><span>Strong</span>I select the main trend and one supporting detail.</p>
  <p class="lesson-fix-line"><strong>Fix:</strong> Selection is part of the skill.</p>
</article>
</div>

## Why It Matters
PTE Core is scored through computer-delivered tasks, but the human skill underneath is still clear communication. When you understand Describe Image organization, you waste less time guessing what the task wants. You can focus on meaning, structure, grammar, pronunciation, or listening accuracy in the exact way the task requires. This makes practice more efficient and makes the simulator feel less intimidating.
