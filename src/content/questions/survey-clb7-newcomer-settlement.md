## Your task

Complete a **newcomer settlement services survey**. You must:

- Comment on language support, employment help, housing guidance, and overall experience
- Give honest, specific feedback in each section
- Suggest one concrete improvement
- Use a respectful, constructive tone

**Word count target:** 140–170 words

---

## Model answer (CLB 7)

> **Newcomer Settlement Services Survey — Bridgeview Immigration Services**
>
> **Language Support**
> Very good. The ESL classes were well-organized and the instructors were patient. The beginner and intermediate levels were clearly different, which helped me learn faster.
>
> **Employment Help**
> Fair. The resume workshop was useful, but the job search support ended after two sessions. I would have liked more ongoing help, especially with job interview preparation.
>
> **Housing Guidance**
> Good. The staff explained the rental process clearly and gave me a helpful checklist. I felt much less confused after the orientation.
>
> **Overall Experience**
> I felt welcomed and supported. The staff were friendly and easy to reach by phone or email.
>
> **One Suggested Improvement**
> I suggest adding a mock interview workshop to the employment program. Many newcomers, including myself, have difficulty with job interviews in English. Practising with a counsellor before a real interview would make a big difference.

## Why this scores CLB 7

| CLB Criterion | What this response does well |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | All four sections rated with specific feedback; one suggestion provided |
| **Detail** | Class levels noted, resume workshop mentioned, checklist referenced |
| **Organization** | Follows survey structure clearly; suggestion is specific and explained |
| **Tone** | Honest but positive — balanced feedback, not a complaint |
| **Vocabulary** | "well-organized," "mock interview," "ongoing help," "orientation" |
| **Grammar** | Accurate simple sentences; basic subordination |

---

## Common mistakes at CLB 5–6

| Weak version | Why it loses marks |
|---|---|
| "Services were OK." | No specific detail in any category |
| Skipping a section | All four areas are required |
| "Everything was bad." | No nuance; unbalanced responses score lower |
| Suggestion with no reason | At CLB 7, your suggestion should include one sentence of explanation |

---

## Examiner tip

Settlement surveys are personal, but they should not sound like a diary entry. Think of each section as a **mini-report**: what was the service, how did it help, what was missing. The most memorable CLB 7 answers are honest and specific — they name what worked AND what could be better. That balance shows mature communication judgment.
