  see §1) — nothing close to justifying a 4-5x jump in pageviews/visitors.

  concentration) is more consistent with bot/crawler traffic, a referral
  spam wave, or a tracking artifact than real visitors. Recommend checking
  the GA4 Traffic Acquisition report by channel/source for this week before
  treating the volume increase as a signal of anything.

  non-zero event in the snapshots I have** (1 event / 59 visits, 1.69%),
  after being stuck at 0% since week 31. Small sample, but it's the first

  Ebook, AI feedback, and subscription checkout clicks remain at 0 events
  despite generous visit volume — still worth digging into whether this is
  a tracking/pixel issue or a genuine conversion problem, since the visit
  counts (51, 73, 52) are large enough that 0 events across the board is
  unusual on its own.
- As always: organic clicks are 1-3/week, so none of this GA4 movement is
