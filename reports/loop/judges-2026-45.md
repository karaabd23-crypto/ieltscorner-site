## Reasons
- **/ test taker 4:** "Which test are you preparing for?" with three clear exam cards is understood in seconds. **Conversion 3:** the first block is a returning-student booking offer (CA$55 per session), so a new visitor's next step is unclear. Design 4: clean, but cards have empty space. Honesty 4: price stated plainly.
- **/ebook/:** Test taker 4: cover, "all 8 speaking tasks", 144 pages, 24 scored samples and the price CA$49.50 on the button are visible at once. Conversion 4: one dark CTA with price, instant download stated. Design 4: single hero, no clutter. Honesty 4: facts about the product only, no score promise seen; PhD-led teaching and TESL Canada line match the owner's credentials.
- **/ai-feedback/ test taker 3:** the tool is above the fold on desktop, but "0 essays submitted today" signals an unused tool and the framing "supports writing development, not just a score" is vague about what you get. Conversion 3: two CTAs, no paid offer visible at the top (the free score hook is good). Design 4. Honesty 4: no guarantee, correctly says feedback, not an official score. Basis of the chosen 2026-45 change.
- **/celpip/reading/ test taker 4:** facts tile (4 parts, about 55 to 60 minutes, 38 questions) is useful and calm. Conversion 3: three stacked buttons (Start Free Test, Unlock premium now, Book private coaching) with equal weight on the secondary two; "Unlock premium" gives no price. Design 4. Honesty 4.
- **/tutoring/ test taker 3:** hero shows only a small button, no price or session length in the first screen; the three-step explanation is clear. Conversion 3: price not near the button (CA$55 appears on the homepage, not here in the first screen). Design 4. Honesty 4: no promised score jump seen ("find the exact errors"). Page weight about 10 MB (backlog e) hurts phone users.
- **/pte-core/simulator/ test taker 3:** purpose is clear, but a fresh visitor sees a "116:00" timer and "Premium attempts unlocked". Conversion 2: nothing to buy or sign up for visible, because the gate is switched off. **Honesty 2:** "Premium attempts unlocked" is shown to everyone because `hasPremiumSession()` returns true ("temporary open access"). Design 4. Needs-Kay decision (backlog 12).
