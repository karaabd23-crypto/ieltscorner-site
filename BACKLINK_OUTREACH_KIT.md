# Backlink Outreach Kit (ieltscorner.ca)

Backlinks are the audit's highest-impact, still-unstarted lever. The work itself
is off-site (email, directory submissions, guest posts) and must be done by a
human. This kit makes that fast: a prioritized target list, ready-to-send
templates, and the linkable assets to point them at.

Created 2026-06-02. Owner action required (Claude cannot send email or submit forms).

---

## Your best linkable assets (link these, not the homepage)

People link to *tools and resources*, not sales pages. Lead every pitch with one of:

1. **CLB / Band Score Calculator** — `/clb-calculator`
   The single most linkable page. Immigration forums and blogs link to score
   converters because their readers constantly ask "what's my CLB?".
2. **Free CELPIP Reading Test** — `/celpip/reading/free-test`
   A real, free, no-signup diagnostic. Strong for "free CELPIP practice" lists.
3. **IELTS vs CELPIP vs PTE Core comparison** — `/ielts-vs-celpip-vs-pte-core`
   Definitive comparison; immigration blogs cite these to answer "which test?".
4. **CELPIP for Canadian Immigration** — `/celpip-for-canadian-immigration`
   CLB requirements for Express Entry / PNP / citizenship. Cite-able reference.

## Tier 1 targets — Canadian immigration media (highest authority)

| Target | Angle | How to reach |
|--------|-------|--------------|
| CIC News (cicnews.com) | Offer an expert quote or a data-backed guest piece on language-test requirements | Editorial contact form / tips email |
| Moving2Canada | Resource-list inclusion (calculator + free test) | Partnerships/contact form |
| Settlement.org | Newcomer resource directory listing | Suggest-a-resource form |
| Canadavisa forums | Helpful answers linking the calculator where relevant (no spam) | Community account |

## Tier 2 targets — directories & marketplaces (easy wins, do first)

| Target | Action |
|--------|--------|
| TESL Canada educator/member directory | Submit profile with site link |
| Google Business Profile | Claim/verify; add site link |
| Tutoring marketplaces: Wyzant, TutorOcean, Tutorax | Create tutor profile linking site |
| Local newcomer settlement agencies (city-level) | Offer as a free resource for their clients |

## Tier 3 targets — communities (slow, relationship-based)

- r/CELPIP, r/ImmigrationCanada, r/PTE — be genuinely helpful; link the
  calculator/free test only where it answers the actual question. Self-promo
  rules are strict; build karma first.
- CELPIP/IELTS Facebook study groups — same principle.

## Suggested order (first 2 weeks)

1. Tier 2 directories + Google Business Profile (fast, no reply needed).
2. 5 Tier 1 resource-list emails (template A) to pages that already mention CLB.
3. 1 guest-post pitch (template B) to CIC News or Moving2Canada.
4. Start being active in 1–2 communities (no links yet; build credibility).
