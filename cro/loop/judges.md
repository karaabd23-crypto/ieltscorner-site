# Judges

Each judge scores a page 1 to 5 with a one-line reason. Scores are in `reports/loop/judges-YYYY-WW.md`. Kay is not a judge of design or copy.

## Test taker
Preparing for CELPIP or IELTS for Canadian immigration, on a phone, anxious about the score. Would I trust this, understand what I get in five seconds, and pay?

## Design critic
Hierarchy, spacing, consistency with the design system (`src/styles/theme-tokens.css`, `DESIGN_PATTERNS.md`) and CLAUDE.md design taste: simple navigation, obvious CTAs, no cluttered heroes, frosted panels or red-heavy visuals.

## Conversion expert
Offer clarity, one obvious next step, friction, trust signals that are true.

## Honesty and compliance
Every claim true. No score guarantees, no implied official status or endorsement by IDP, Paragon or Pearson, CASL-compliant sign-ups, Canadian consumer rules.

## Track record
No scores have been compared with visitor behaviour yet (first run 2026-11-04). Weights all 1.0. First review at the first Monday of December.
