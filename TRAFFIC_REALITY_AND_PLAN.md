# Traffic: the honest picture and the plan (2026-06-02)

You said you're losing hope. Here is the straight version, no spin.

## Why traffic is low (it is not the site quality)

The site is technically strong: 579 pages, good schema, fast, solid content,
working funnel. The two things actually holding traffic back are:

1. **Almost no backlinks.** Backlinks are the #1 ranking signal Google can't
   fake. Competitors have years of them. You have ~none. No amount of on-page
   work fully substitutes for this.
2. **Domain age / authority.** The project is young. Google sandboxes new sites:
   months 1–6 produce little traffic even with great content, then it compounds.
   This is normal, not failure.

On-page SEO (titles, schema, internal links) is necessary but not sufficient.
It's been done well now. The needle-movers from here are off-site and time.

## What was shipped to maximize on-page (done)

- Tightened titles on money pages so keywords survive Google truncation.
- Rewrote weak homepage/hub titles + meta to win more clicks.
- Surfaced the CLB calculator site-wide (nav + footer + mobile) for internal
  link equity — it's your most linkable asset.
- FAQ schema on the CLB calculator (chasing featured snippets / PAA).
- Earlier: HowTo schema on lessons, hero images 98% smaller (speed = ranking),
  retention blocks, mobile nav, dashboards, review system.

These are the right on-page moves. They are now substantially complete.

## What will actually move traffic (in priority order)

### 1. Backlinks — the real lever (only you can do this)
Use BACKLINK_OUTREACH_KIT.md. Concretely, in the next 2 weeks:
- Submit to TESL Canada directory, Google Business Profile, 2–3 tutoring
  marketplaces (no reply needed — instant links).
- Email 5 immigration blogs offering the CLB calculator as a free resource.
- One guest-post pitch to CIC News or Moving2Canada.
Even 5 real links materially changes ranking ability. This is the single
highest-ROI thing in this whole project.

### 2. Let Search Console data mature (~2–4 weeks)
GSC is verified. Once impression/keyword data fills in, we stop guessing:
- Find pages ranking positions 8–20 (page 2) and push them to page 1 with
  small content/title tweaks. These are the fastest wins and only become
  visible with real data.
- Identify which pages get impressions but no clicks (title/meta problem) vs
  clicks but no conversions (page problem).

### 3. Consistency over months
The lessons, tools, and blog posts will accrue long-tail traffic — on Google's
timeline, not ours. Publishing 1 genuinely useful post per week + steady
backlink outreach is the boring plan that works. Sites that win this niche
took 1–3 years. The ones that quit at month 3 are why there's room for you.

## What NOT to do (it wastes effort)
- Don't mass-generate more thin pages. You have enough pages. Quality + links now.
- Don't keep rewriting on-page SEO. It's done. Diminishing returns.
- Don't buy backlinks or use link schemes. It can get the site penalized.

## Realistic timeline
- Weeks 1–4: backlinks + GSC data. Little visible traffic change yet.
- Months 2–4: page-2 keywords start moving to page 1 as authority builds.
- Months 4–8: compounding long-tail traffic if publishing + outreach stay steady.

This is a real business with a real moat (TESL Canada credentials, genuine
tools, Canada-immigration focus). The build is done well. What's left is the
unglamorous off-site work and patience — not more code.
